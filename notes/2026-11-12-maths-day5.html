Topic 1: Algebra Basics — Short Notes

  • A variable is a letter (like x or y) that stands for an unknown number. A constant is a fixed number. In 3x + 5, 3 is the coefficient of x and 5 is the constant. Harf (x, y) variable hota hai, number constant hota hai.
  • Like terms have the same letters with the same powers: 3x and 5x are like terms, but 3x and 3x2 are not. Add or subtract like terms only: 3x + 5x = 8x.
  • To find the value of an expression, put the number in place of the letter: if x = 3, then 2x + 5 = 2 × 3 + 5 = 11.
  • To solve an equation, do the same thing on both sides: x + 7 = 15 → x = 15 − 7 = 8. (Add ho to minus, multiply ho to divide — ulta amal karo.)
  • Powers: x2 × x3 = x5 (powers add when multiplying). x2 means x × x.
  • Factorise means take out the common factor: 6x + 12 = 6(x + 2). Check by multiplying back: 6 × x + 6 × 2 = 6x + 12.

Topic 2: Geometry Basics — Short Notes

  • An acute angle is less than 90°, a right angle is 90°, an obtuse angle is between 90° and 180°, and a straight angle is 180°.
  • Complementary angles add up to 90°. Supplementary angles add up to 180°. Vertically opposite angles (aamne-saamne) are equal.
  • Angles in a triangle add up to 180°. Angles in a quadrilateral add up to 360°.
  • Rectangle: Perimeter = 2 × (length + width); Area = length × width. Square: Perimeter = 4 × side; Area = side × side.
  • Triangle: Area = (1/2) × base × height. Circle: Circumference = 2 × pi × radius; Area = pi × radius × radius (pi = 22/7).
  • Pythagoras theorem (right-angled triangle): (hypotenuse)2 = (base)2 + (perpendicular)2. Example: 3, 4, 5 — because 32 + 42 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 52.
