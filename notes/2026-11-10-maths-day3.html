Topic 1: Profit & Loss — Short Notes

  • Cost Price (CP) = khareedne ki qeemat. Selling Price (SP) = bechne ki qeemat. Profit = SP − CP (jab SP > CP). Loss = CP − SP (jab SP < CP). Faida tab hota hai jab bechne ki qeemat khareedne se zyada ho.
  • Profit% = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100. Loss% = (Loss ÷ CP) × 100. Percent hamesha CP par nikalte hain, SP par nahi.
  • SP nikalna: SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) ÷ 100. Loss ho to SP = CP × (100 − Loss%) ÷ 100.
  • CP nikalna: CP = SP × 100 ÷ (100 + Profit%). Loss ho to CP = SP × 100 ÷ (100 − Loss%).
  • Discount = Marked Price − Selling Price. Discount% hamesha marked price par nikalte hain.
  • Do cheezein aik hi SP par bechi jayein — aik par x% faida, dusri par x% nuqsan — to hamesha nuqsan hota hai: Loss% = x² ÷ 100.

Topic 2: Simple Interest — Short Notes

  • Simple Interest (SI) = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100. Principal (P) = asal raqam, Rate (R) = saalana %, Time (T) = saal (years). Simple interest mein har saal aik hi raqam par interest milta hai.
  • Amount (Miqdar) = Principal + Interest. A = P + SI.
  • Rate nikalna: R = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × T). Time nikalna: T = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × R).
  • Principal nikalna: P = (SI × 100) ÷ (R × T).
  • Time hamesha saal (years) mein ho — months hon to 12 se taqseem karo. 6 months = 0.5 year, 3 months = 0.25 year.
  • Rate hamesha % per year (per annum) hota hai. Agar raqam dugni (double) ho jaye to SI = P hota hai.
