Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Number System: sab se chhota composite number 4 hai; 2 waahid even prime number hai; do numbers ka product = HCF × LCM; pehle n natural numbers ka sum = n × (n + 1) / 2 (pehle 15 numbers ka sum = 120).
  • Sets & Statistics: A ∪ B = dono sets ke tamam elements, A ∩ B = sirf mushtarka elements; n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B); 4 elements wale set ke 16 subsets hote hain (2^4); mean = sum ÷ count, median = beech wali value, mode = sab se zyada aane wali value, range = bara − chhota.
  • Pakistan: Pakistan ka doosra bara sehra — Cholistan; sab se uncha darra — Khunjerab Pass; Pakistan ne atomic tests 28 May 1998 ko Chagai mein kiye; National Assembly ki pehli khatoon Speaker — Fahmida Mirza.
  • Islamiat: Ghazwa-e-Hunayn aur Fatah-e-Makkah dono 8 Hijri mein huay; Aam-ul-Huzn (Gham ka Saal) Nabuwat ka 10th saal tha; Mu'awwidhatayn = Surah Al-Falaq aur Surah An-Nas; Surah Ar-Rahman ko “Bride of the Quran” kaha jata hai.
