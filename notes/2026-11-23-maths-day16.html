Topic 1: Ratio & Proportion — Short Notes

  • A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind. a : b means a/b. Both must be in the same unit — 30 cm : 1 m = 30 : 100 = 3 : 10. Ratio do cheezon ka muqabla hai, dono ki unit aik jaisi honi chahiye.
  • Simplify a ratio: divide both parts by their HCF. 18 : 24 ÷ 6 = 3 : 4.
  • Proportion: a : b = c : d means a/b = c/d. Cross-multiply: a × d = b × c. Example: 5 : 6 = 25 : 30 because 5 × 30 = 6 × 25 = 150.
  • Dividing a quantity in a ratio: add the parts, then each share = (its part ÷ total parts) × total. Rs 1,500 in 3 : 2 → parts 5, each part 300, shares 900 and 600.
  • Unitary method: first find the value of ONE unit, then multiply. If 6 books cost Rs 180, 1 book = Rs 30, so 10 books = Rs 300.
  • If a : b = 3 : 4 and b : c = 2 : 5, then a : c = (3 × 2) : (4 × 5) = 6 : 20 = 3 : 10.

Topic 2: Average — Short Notes

  • Average = (sum of all values) ÷ (number of values). Average nikalne ke liye sab ko jama kar ke gin-ti se taqseem karo.
  • Sum = average × number of values. If the average of 7 numbers is 14, their sum is 7 × 14 = 98.
  • Average of first n natural numbers = (n + 1) ÷ 2. First 6 natural numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6): (6 + 1)/2 = 3.5.
  • Average of consecutive numbers = the middle number. 5, 15, 25, 35 → middle = 20.
  • Average of numbers that are all the same = that number. 3, 3, 3, 3, 3 → average 3.
  • When a new value joins the group, first find the old sum, add the new value, then divide by the new count.
