Topic 1: Ratio & Proportion — Short Notes

  • A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind. a : b means a/b. Both must be in the same unit — 25 cm : 1 m = 25 : 100 = 1 : 4. Ratio do cheezon ka muqabla hai, dono ki unit aik jaisi honi chahiye.
  • Simplify a ratio: divide both parts by their HCF. 12 : 16 ÷ 4 = 3 : 4.
  • Proportion: a : b = c : d means a/b = c/d. Cross-multiply: a × d = b × c. Example: 3 : 4 = 15 : 20 because 3 × 20 = 4 × 15 = 60.
  • Dividing a quantity in a ratio: add the parts, then each share = (its part ÷ total parts) × total. Rs 900 in 2 : 3 → parts 5, each part 180, shares 360 and 540.
  • Unitary method: first find the value of ONE unit, then multiply. If 5 pens cost Rs 125, 1 pen = Rs 25, so 8 pens = Rs 200.
  • If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, then a : c = (2 × 4) : (3 × 5) = 8 : 15.

Topic 2: Average — Short Notes

  • Average = (sum of all values) ÷ (number of values). Average nikalne ke liye sab ko jama kar ke gin-ti se taqseem karo.
  • Sum = average × number of values. If the average of 5 numbers is 20, their sum is 5 × 20 = 100.
  • Average of first n natural numbers = (n + 1) ÷ 2. First 5 natural numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, 5): (5 + 1)/2 = 3.
  • Average of consecutive numbers = the middle number. 10, 20, 30, 40 → middle = 25.
  • Average of numbers that are all the same = that number. 8, 8, 8, 8 → average 8.
  • When a new value joins the group, first find the old sum, add the new value, then divide by the new count.
