Mathematics Book — Test Preparation Notes & MCQs

Mathematics · SPSC / FPSC / Teaching & Screening Tests · By MR Academy

This book covers the maths topics that come again and again in screening tests: percentages, ratio, average, profit and loss, speed and basic algebra. Every section is written in simple words with one easy formula, so a student who is weak in maths can also prepare step by step.

Percentages

Percent means “out of one hundred”. To change a fraction into percent, multiply by 100. For example, 1/2 × 100 = 50%. To find a percent of a number, use part = percent × total ÷ 100. So 20% of 150 = 20 × 150 ÷ 100 = 30.

  • Percent increase = increase ÷ old value × 100.
  • Percent decrease = decrease ÷ old value × 100.
  • 50% means half, 25% means one quarter, 10% means one tenth — remembering these saves time in the test hall.

Ratio & Proportion

A ratio compares two quantities, written like 2 : 3. If a sum of money is divided in the ratio 2 : 3, first add the parts: 2 + 3 = 5 parts. Each share is then found by dividing the total by 5 and multiplying by its part.

A proportion is an equality of two ratios, for example 2/3 = 4/6. In proportion questions, cross-multiplication is the safe method: if a/b = c/d, then a × d = b × c. Always write the same units on both sides before solving.

Average

The average (mean) of some numbers is sum of values ÷ number of values. The average of 4, 6 and 8 is (4 + 6 + 8) ÷ 3 = 6.

  • If the average and the count are given, the total = average × count.
  • When a new value joins the group, add it to the old total and divide by the new count.
  • The average of the first n natural numbers is (n + 1) ÷ 2.

Profit & Loss

Cost price (CP) is what a shopkeeper pays; selling price (SP) is what he sells for. If SP is greater than CP, there is a profit = SP − CP. If CP is greater, there is a loss = CP − SP.

Profit or loss percent is always calculated on the cost price: profit % = profit ÷ CP × 100. Example: an item bought for Rs 400 and sold for Rs 460 gives a profit of Rs 60, and 60 ÷ 400 × 100 = 15% profit.

Time, Speed & Distance

These three are joined by one relation: distance = speed × time. From this, speed = distance ÷ time, and time = distance ÷ speed.

  • To change km/h into m/s, multiply by 5/18. To change m/s into km/h, multiply by 18/5.
  • A train crosses a pole in the time it takes to travel its own length.
  • If two objects move in opposite directions, their speeds are added; if they move in the same direction, the speeds are subtracted.

Basic Algebra

In algebra, letters stand for unknown numbers. The job is to find the letter's value. Keep the balance: whatever you do to one side of the equation, do the same to the other side.

  • If x + 7 = 15, subtract 7 from both sides: x = 8.
  • If 3x = 21, divide both sides by 3: x = 7.
  • Useful identities: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b² and a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b).
  • Like terms can be added: 4x + 3x = 7x. Unlike terms, such as 4x and 3y, cannot be joined.

Practice MCQs — 15 Questions

Try these first, then check the answer key at the end.

  1. What is 25% of 200?
    (a) 25 (b) 50 (c) 75 (d) 100
  2. 40 is what percent of 80?
    (a) 40% (b) 50% (c) 60% (d) 80%
  3. A sum is divided in the ratio 2 : 3. If the total is 50, the larger share is:
    (a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 35
  4. If a : b = 3 : 4 and a = 12, then b = ?
    (a) 14 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 20
  5. The average of 10, 20, 30 and 40 is:
    (a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 20 (d) 35
  6. The average of the first five even numbers (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) is:
    (a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 8
  7. An article is bought for Rs 500 and sold for Rs 600. The profit is:
    (a) Rs 50 (b) Rs 100 (c) Rs 150 (d) Rs 200
  8. An article is bought for Rs 800 and sold for Rs 720. The loss percent is:
    (a) 5% (b) 8% (c) 10% (d) 12%
  9. A car runs at 60 km/h. The distance it covers in 2 hours is:
    (a) 120 km (b) 100 km (c) 90 km (d) 62 km
  10. A bus covers 150 km in 3 hours. Its speed is:
    (a) 40 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 45 km/h
  11. If x + 5 = 12, then x = ?
    (a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 8
  12. If 2x = 18, then x = ?
    (a) 9 (b) 8 (c) 7 (d) 6
  13. The value of 3² + 4² is:
    (a) 20 (b) 23 (c) 24 (d) 25
  14. A price rises from Rs 50 to Rs 75. The percent increase is:
    (a) 25% (b) 50% (c) 75% (d) 100%
  15. The average of 5 numbers is 20. Their sum is:
    (a) 80 (b) 90 (c) 100 (d) 120
