Pakistan Studies — Provinces of Pakistan

Pakistan Studies Provinces of Pakistan 10 Practice MCQs

Pakistan's four provinces, Gilgit-Baltistan, Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Islamabad — their capitals, languages, area and population, and the facts tests ask again and again — in short simple notes with Roman Urdu explanations, then 10 practice MCQs with an answer key.

Pakistan at a Glance

  • Pakistan has four provinces — Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan — plus two territories, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and the Islamabad Capital Territory. Roman Urdu: Pakistan ke chaar sube, do ilaqay aur Islamabad hain.
  • Total area: about 881,913 square kilometres. Population: about 241.5 million (2023 census) — the fifth largest population in the world.
  • National language: Urdu. Each province has its own main language — Punjabi in Punjab, Sindhi in Sindh, Pashto in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochi and Brahui in Balochistan.
  • Every province has its own provincial assembly and a Chief Minister. The 18th Amendment (2010) gave the provinces more powers of their own.

Quick Table — Provinces and Territories

  • Punjab — Capital: Lahore — Language: Punjabi — Largest province by population (about 127 million).
  • Sindh — Capital: Karachi — Language: Sindhi — Pakistan's largest city and main seaport.
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — Capital: Peshawar — Language: Pashto — Old name: NWFP (renamed in 2010).
  • Balochistan — Capital: Quetta — Languages: Balochi, Brahui, Pashto — Largest province by area (about 44% of Pakistan).
  • Gilgit-Baltistan — Capital: Gilgit — Languages: Shina, Balti — Home of K2, the world's second-highest mountain.
  • Azad Jammu and Kashmir — Capital: Muzaffarabad — Language: Pahari / Urdu — The Mangla Dam is built here, on the Jhelum River.
  • Islamabad Capital Territory — Capital: Islamabad — The federal capital of Pakistan.

1. Punjab — The Land of Five Rivers

  • Punjab means "land of five waters" — its five rivers are the Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas and Sutlej. The Indus is NOT one of the five rivers of Punjab. Roman Urdu: Punjab ka matlab hai paanch dariyaon ki zameen.
  • It is the largest province by population (about 127 million in the 2023 census) and the centre of Pakistan's farming — wheat, cotton and rice.
  • Capital Lahore is called the "City of Gardens" and the cultural heart of Pakistan. Other big cities: Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan and Gujranwala.

2. Sindh — Bab-ul-Islam

  • Sindh is called "Bab-ul-Islam" — the Gateway of Islam — because Islam first came to South Asia here, when Muhammad bin Qasim arrived in 712 AD.
  • Capital Karachi is Pakistan's largest city, its main seaport and its business centre. It was Pakistan's first capital (1947–1959).
  • The Indus River flows through Sindh into the Arabian Sea. The Thar Desert lies in eastern Sindh, and the Manchar Lake — Pakistan's largest lake — is also in Sindh.

3. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — The North-West

  • Its old name was the North-West Frontier Province (NWFP). It was renamed Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 2010 through the 18th Amendment.
  • Capital Peshawar is one of the oldest cities of South Asia. The Khyber Pass connects Peshawar with Afghanistan.
  • The province is famous for its mountains and valleys — Swat, Chitral and Abbottabad — and for the Karakoram Highway that begins here and runs to China.

4. Balochistan — The Largest by Area

  • Balochistan is the largest province by area — about 347,190 square kilometres, nearly 44% of Pakistan — but the smallest by population (about 15 million).
  • Capital Quetta. Its largest district is Chagai. The Gwadar port on the Arabian Sea and the Sui gas field (gas first found in 1952) are both in Balochistan.
  • Remember the pair: largest by AREA = Balochistan, largest by POPULATION = Punjab. Tests ask this pair again and again.

5. Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir

  • Gilgit-Baltistan — capital Gilgit. It holds K2 (8,611 metres), the second-highest mountain in the world, and the Khunjerab Pass to China.
  • Azad Jammu and Kashmir — capital Muzaffarabad. The Mangla Dam on the Jhelum River and the beautiful Neelum Valley are here.
  • Islamabad became the federal capital in 1967, replacing Karachi. It is a planned city at the foot of the Margalla Hills.

Common Test Traps

  • Largest by area = Balochistan; largest by population = Punjab. Do not mix the two.
  • Karachi is the capital of Sindh — it is NOT the national capital any more. The national capital is Islamabad.
  • The five rivers of Punjab are Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej — the Indus is NOT one of them.
  • Old name of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa = NWFP; renamed in 2010 (18th Amendment).
  • Capitals to remember: Punjab — Lahore; Sindh — Karachi; Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — Peshawar; Balochistan — Quetta; Gilgit-Baltistan — Gilgit; Azad Kashmir — Muzaffarabad.

    (a) Peshawar   (b) Quetta   (c) Lahore   (d) Karachi
  2. Which province has the largest population in Pakistan?
    (a) Sindh   (b) Balochistan   (c) Khyber Pakhtunkhwa   (d) Punjab
  3. What was the old name of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?

  4. Which river is NOT one of the five rivers of Punjab?
    (a) Jhelum   (b) Chenab   (c) Ravi   (d) Indus
  5. What is the main language of Sindh?
    (a) Punjabi   (b) Pashto   (c) Sindhi   (d) Balochi
  6. The Gwadar port is in which province?
    (a) Balochistan   (b) Sindh   (c) Punjab   (d) Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  7. The Khyber Pass connects Pakistan with which country?
    (a) China   (b) Iran   (c) Afghanistan   (d) India
  8. Which province is called "Bab-ul-Islam" (the Gateway of Islam)?
    (a) Punjab   (b) Sindh   (c) Balochistan   (d) Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  9. What is the capital of Gilgit-Baltistan?
    (a) Gilgit   (b) Skardu   (c) Hunza   (d) Chitral

    (a) 2008   (b) 2010   (c) 1973   (d) 2018
