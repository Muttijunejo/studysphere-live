English — Articles & Prepositions

English Articles & Prepositions 10 Practice MCQs

When to use a, an and the, in / on / at for time and place, and the most-tested preposition phrases — short, simple notes in easy English with Roman Urdu explanations, then 10 practice MCQs with an answer key.

Articles — A, An, The

  • A / An — use before a singular countable noun when it is mentioned for the first time, or when it is one of many. Roman Urdu: jab kisi cheez ka pehli dafa zikr ho, ya woh aik aam cheez ho — a book, a teacher.
  • Use an before a word that starts with a vowel SOUND (a, e, i, o, u sound) — an apple, an hour (h is silent), an honest man. Use a before a consonant sound — a book, a university (u sounds like ‘yu’), a one-rupee coin.
  • Use the when the noun is specific or already known — the book on the table, the sun, the moon, the Holy Quran, the Prime Minister of Pakistan. Roman Urdu: jab cheez makhsoos ho ya pehle se maloom ho — the.
  • No article before plural nouns in a general sense (Books are useful), before names of people, most countries and cities (Ali, Pakistan, Lahore), before meals (breakfast, lunch), and before subjects and languages (English, Mathematics).
  • Test trap: the is used before rivers, seas, oceans, deserts and mountain ranges — the Indus, the Arabian Sea, the Thar Desert, the Himalayas — but NOT before single mountains or lakes — Mount Everest, Lake Manchar.

Prepositions of Time — In, On, At

  • At — exact time: at 5 o’clock, at noon, at night, at midnight. Roman Urdu: bilkul waqt ke liye — at.
  • On — days and dates: on Monday, on 12 November, on Eid day, on Sunday morning.
  • In — longer periods: in 2026, in November, in the morning, in summer, in the 21st century.
  • Remember the set: in the morning / afternoon / evening, but at night. On time = bilkul waqt par; in time = waqt se pehle ya waqt par (late nahi).

Prepositions of Place — In, On, At

  • In — inside a big place: in Pakistan, in Karachi, in the room, in the box.
  • On — on a surface: on the table, on the wall, on the first floor, on the bus (when you are on it).
  • At — a small, exact point: at the door, at school, at the bus stop, at home.
  • By — travel: by bus, by train, by car, by air — but on foot (never ‘by foot’). Go home takes no preposition — never ‘go to home’.

Fixed Preposition Phrases — Yaad Karne Wale

  • afraid of (She is afraid of dogs) · good at (He is good at English) · interested in (I am interested in science) · depend on (We depend on rain) · married to (She is married to Ali).
  • angry with a person, angry at a thing · sorry for a mistake · look at / listen to / laugh at · arrive at a small place, arrive in a big place (arrive at school, arrive in Lahore).
  • Since vs For: since + a point in time (since 2015, since Monday, since morning); for + a length of time (for two years, for three days, for a long time).
  • Of shows belonging or material: the capital of Pakistan, a cup of tea, made of wood. To shows direction or giving: go to school, give it to me, listen to me.

Common Test Traps

  • ‘An’ follows the SOUND, not the letter: an hour, an honest man, an M.A. (em sound) — but a university, a one-rupee note, a European (consonant sound).
  • Never use ‘the’ before a person’s name or before a subject: ‘The Ali is my friend’ is wrong; ‘The English is easy’ is wrong.
  • ‘In’ + year/month/season, ‘on’ + day/date, ‘at’ + clock time — the most repeated article/preposition test pattern.
  • ‘By’ + transport, ‘on’ + foot; ‘at’ night but ‘in’ the morning; ‘go home’ with no ‘to’.

Practice MCQs — Articles & Prepositions (Q1–Q10)

  1. ___ honest man is respected by all.
    (a) The   (b) A   (c) No article   (d) An
  2. She was born ___ 12 November 2000.
    (a) in   (b) on   (c) at   (d) of
  3. He is good ___ English.
    (a) at   (b) in   (c) on   (d) of
  4. The train arrived ___ 5 o’clock.
    (a) in   (b) on   (c) at   (d) by
  5. I am interested ___ science.
    (a) in   (b) on   (c) at   (d) with
  6. She is afraid ___ dogs.
    (a) from   (b) of   (c) with   (d) by
  7. ___ sun rises in the east.
    (a) A   (b) An   (c) The   (d) No article
  8. He went to school ___ bus.
    (a) by   (b) in   (c) on   (d) with
  9. This book is ___ the table.
    (a) in   (b) at   (c) of   (d) on
  10. They have lived here ___ 2015.
    (a) for   (b) from   (c) since   (d) at
