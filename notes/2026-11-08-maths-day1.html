Topic 1: Fractions & Decimals — Short Notes

  • A fraction is a part of a whole. In 3/4, the top number (3) is the numerator and the bottom number (4) is the denominator. Upar wala numerator, neeche wala denominator kehlata hai.
  • Proper fraction: numerator < denominator (3/4). Improper: numerator ≥ denominator (7/4). Mixed number: 1⅓ = 7/4.
  • Simplify: divide top and bottom by their HCF. Example: 18/24 ÷ 6 = 3/4.
  • Add / subtract with the same denominator: add only the numerators. With different denominators, first make them equal using the LCM. 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
  • Multiply: numerator × numerator, denominator × denominator. 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5.
  • Divide: multiply by the reciprocal (second fraction ulta kar ke). 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 3/2.
  • Fraction ↔ decimal: divide numerator by denominator (3/8 = 0.375). Decimal → fraction: put over 10, 100 or 1000 and simplify (0.6 = 6/10 = 3/5).

Topic 2: Percentages — Short Notes

  • Percent means “out of 100”. 25% = 25/100 = 1/4. Percent ka matlab hai “100 mein se”.
  • % → decimal: divide by 100 (45% = 0.45). Decimal → %: multiply by 100 (0.6 = 60%).
  • Fraction → %: multiply by 100. 3/5 = 3/5 × 100 = 60%.
  • % of a number: (percent × number) ÷ 100. 20% of 150 = 20 × 150 ÷ 100 = 30.
  • Increase: new value = old × (1 + %/100). 200 increased by 10% = 200 × 1.10 = 220.
  • Decrease: new value = old × (1 − %/100). 200 decreased by 10% = 200 × 0.90 = 180.
  • Finding the %: (part ÷ whole) × 100. 30 out of 120 = 30/120 × 100 = 25%.
