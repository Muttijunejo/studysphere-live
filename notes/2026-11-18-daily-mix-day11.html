Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Time & Work: 1-day work = 1 ÷ din; dono mil kar = (x × y) ÷ (x + y) din. Zyada aadmi → kam din (Men × Days barabar rehta hai).
  • Time, Speed & Distance: Speed = Distance ÷ Time; km/h → m/s × 5/18; m/s → km/h × 18/5; dono taraf barabar fasla ho to Average Speed = 2xy ÷ (x + y).
  • Pakistan: pehli khatoon Governor Sindh — Ra'ana Liaquat Ali Khan; pehle Chief of Army Staff — General Frank Messervy; sab se bara dam — Tarbela; pehle Law Minister — Jogendra Nath Mandal.
  • Science: Universal Donor = O negative, Universal Recipient = AB positive; Nucleus cell ka control centre hai (genetic material wahin hota hai); Protein muscles banata hai, Carbohydrate energy deta hai.
  • Sindhi: Father = Pee (پيءُ), Sister = Bhen (ڀيڻ), Tree = Wanh (وڻ), Bird = Paki (پکي), Dog = Kuto (ڪتو), Horse = Ghoṛo (گهوڙو), Son = Putt (پٽ), Daughter = Dhee (ڌيءُ), Day = Dinh (ڏينهن), Earth = Dharti (ڌرتي).
