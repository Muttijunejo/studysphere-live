  • Maths: Profit = SP − CP; Loss = CP − SP; profit % hamesha CP par nikalte hain — 1,800 → 2,250 = 25% profit; Simple Interest = P × R × T ÷ 100; Amount = P + SI.
  • Revision (Kaizen): 3/4 of 320 = 240; 40% of 250 = 100; A 12 din + B 24 din = aik saath 8 din; side 9 cm wale square ka area = 81 sq cm; 25 = 32.
  • Islamiat: Umm-ul-Kitab — Surah Al-Fatiha; Zabur Hazrat Dawud (AS), Injeel Hazrat Isa (AS), Taurat Hazrat Musa (AS) ko mili; pehli masjid — Masjid Quba; Makki Surahs 86, Madani 28.
  • Pakistan: pehle Chief Justice — Mian Abdul Rashid; pehle President — Iskander Mirza; sab se bara desert — Thar; national flower — Jasmine; Lahore Resolution A. K. Fazlul Huq ne pesh ki.
  • Sindhi: paani = water, kheer = milk, raat = night, subah = morning; Sindhi Pakistan mein Arabic script mein likhi jati hai; Shah Jo Risalo — Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai.
