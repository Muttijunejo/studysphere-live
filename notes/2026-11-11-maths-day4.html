Topic 1: Time & Work — Short Notes

  • If A finishes a work in x days, A's 1-day work = 1 ÷ x. Aik din ka kaam = 1 ÷ din.
  • Work done together in 1 day = 1/x + 1/y. Days together = (x × y) ÷ (x + y). Example: 12 and 18 days → 12 × 18 ÷ 30 = 7.2 days.
  • If A works alone for n days, work done = n ÷ x; work left = 1 − (n ÷ x).
  • More men → fewer days (ulta hisaab): Men1 × Days1 = Men2 × Days2.
  • If A is twice as efficient as B, A takes half the time of B. Thrice as efficient → one-third the time.
  • Wages are shared in the ratio of the work done (1-day work ratio), not in the ratio of days.

Topic 2: Time, Speed & Distance — Short Notes

  • Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Distance = Speed × Time. Time = Distance ÷ Speed.
  • km/h → m/s: multiply by 5/18. m/s → km/h: multiply by 18/5. Example: 72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
  • Average speed (same distance both ways) = 2xy ÷ (x + y). Example: 60 and 40 km/h → 2 × 60 × 40 ÷ 100 = 48 km/h.
  • Relative speed: opposite directions → add; same direction → subtract.
  • Train crossing a pole: time = train length ÷ speed. Crossing a platform: time = (train length + platform length) ÷ speed.
  • Boat: downstream speed = boat speed + stream speed; upstream speed = boat speed − stream speed.
