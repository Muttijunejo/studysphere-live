English Book — Grammar, Vocabulary & MCQs

English · SPSC / FPSC / Teaching & Screening Tests · By MR Academy

This book gives the English grammar and vocabulary that screening tests ask most often: parts of speech, tenses, prepositions, synonyms, antonyms and common errors. The notes are short and simple, with an example for almost every rule, so you can revise quickly before a test.

Parts of Speech (Very Brief)

  • Noun — name of a person, place or thing: teacher, Karachi, book.
  • Pronoun — word used instead of a noun: he, she, it, they.
  • Verb — action word: read, write, run.
  • Adjective — describes a noun: big, honest, three.
  • Adverb — describes a verb: quickly, very, today.
  • Preposition — shows relation: in, on, at, by, with.
  • Conjunction — joins words or sentences: and, but, because.

Tenses

A tense shows the time of an action. Learn these four first:

Present Simple:    I play. / He plays.        (habits, facts)
Past Simple:       I played. / He played.     (finished action)
Present Continuous: I am playing.             (happening now)
Future Simple:     I will play.               (action to come)

Note the small changes: with he/she/it in the present simple we add -s or -es to the verb (he plays, she watches). In the past simple, regular verbs take -ed, but many common verbs are irregular: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, take → took.

Prepositions (Common Pairs)

  • good at — He is good at English. (bad at works the same way)
  • afraid of — She is afraid of dogs.
  • interested in — I am interested in science.
  • married to — He is married to a teacher.
  • depend on — Success depends on hard work.
  • listen to — Listen to your teacher.
  • Use in for months and years (in June, in 2026), on for days (on Monday), and at for exact time (at 9 o'clock).

Synonyms & Antonyms

A synonym has nearly the same meaning; an antonym has the opposite meaning.

Synonyms: happy – joyful · begin – start · brave – bold · rich – wealthy · fast – quick · big – large · end – finish · clever – smart.

Antonyms: brave – cowardly · ancient – modern · honest – dishonest · victory – defeat · expand – contract · arrival – departure · include – exclude · maximum – minimum.

Test tip: in a synonym question, first find the general sense of the word (positive or negative), then choose the closest option.

Common Errors

  • Wrong: He don't like tea. Right: He doesn't like tea. (With he/she/it use does.)
  • Wrong: She is more cleverer. Right: She is cleverer / more clever — never use both together.
  • Wrong: I have seen him yesterday. Right: I saw him yesterday. (Yesterday takes the past simple.)
  • Wrong: The informations are useful. Right: The information is useful. (Information is uncountable.)
  • Wrong: He told that he was busy. Right: He said that he was busy. (Tell needs an object: He told me that…)

One-Word & Idioms Basics

An idiom is a phrase whose meaning is different from its separate words. Common test idioms:

  • Break the ice — to start a conversation in a social setting.
  • Once in a blue moon — very rarely.
  • A blessing in disguise — something that looks bad but turns out good.
  • To beat about the bush — to avoid saying what one means.
  • One-word basics: a person who teaches is a teacher; a place where birds are kept is an aviary; a life story written by oneself is an autobiography.

Practice MCQs — 15 Questions

Try these first, then check the answer key at the end.

  1. Which of the following is a noun?
    (a) run (b) table (c) quickly (d) beautiful
  2. The past tense of “go” is:
    (a) goed (b) went (c) going (d) goes
  3. She ___ to school every day.
    (a) go (b) goes (c) gone (d) going
  4. The synonym of “happy” is:
    (a) joyful (b) sad (c) angry (d) tired
  5. The antonym of “brave” is:
    (a) bold (b) strong (c) cowardly (d) kind
  6. He is good ___ English.
    (a) in (b) at (c) on (d) for
  7. ___ sun rises in the east.
    (a) The (b) A (c) An (d) No article
  8. The plural of “child” is:
    (a) childs (b) childes (c) children (d) childrens
  9. Choose the correct sentence:
    (a) He don't like tea. (b) He doesn't likes tea. (c) He doesn't like tea. (d) He not like tea.
  10. The idiom “break the ice” means:
    (a) to break something frozen (b) to start a conversation (c) to feel very cold (d) to end a meeting
  11. I ___ reading a book now.
    (a) am (b) is (c) are (d) be
  12. The synonym of “begin” is:
    (a) end (b) stop (c) close (d) start
  13. The antonym of “ancient” is:
    (a) old (b) modern (c) early (d) past
  14. The book is ___ the table.
    (a) on (b) between (c) among (d) since
  15. A person who teaches is called a:
    (a) doctor (b) teacher (c) driver (d) farmer
