The Constitution of Pakistan 1973 — Key Facts

Pakistan Studies Constitution 1973 10 Practice MCQs

The Constitution of 1973 is the supreme law of Pakistan and one of the most repeated topics in SPSC and screening tests. Learn its key dates, the structure of Parliament, fundamental rights and the famous articles below.

Back to home page

Key Dates — Pehle Ye Yaad Rakhein

Adopted: 10 April 1973 — passed by the National Assembly.

Came into force: 14 August 1973.

Third Constitution: Pakistan had earlier constitutions in 1956 and 1962; the 1973 Constitution is the one in force today. Roman Urdu: 1973 ka Aain Pakistan ka teesra aur maujooda Aain hai.

Original size: 280 Articles, 12 Parts and 7 Schedules (amendments have added more since).

System: Parliamentary and federal — the Prime Minister runs the government, the President is the head of state. Wazir-e-Azam hukoomat chalata hai, Sadr mulk ka sarbarah hota hai.

Parliament — Majlis-e-Shoora

Parliament has two houses:

National Assembly (lower house) — 336 seats in total: 266 general seats (directly elected), 60 reserved for women and 10 reserved for non-Muslims. Term: 5 years. The Speaker presides over it.

Senate (upper house) — 96 seats. Each Senator serves 6 years, and half the Senate is elected every 3 years. The Chairman presides over it.

Test point: A bill becomes law only after it is passed by both houses and receives the President's assent.

Fundamental Rights — Articles 8 to 28

Articles 8–28 give every citizen basic rights. The most asked ones in tests:

  • Article 9: Security of person — no person shall be deprived of life or liberty except by law.
  • Article 14: Dignity of man — the dignity of every person is inviolable.
  • Article 19: Freedom of speech and expression (with reasonable limits).
  • Article 25: Equality of citizens — all citizens are equal before the law.
  • Article 25-A: Free and compulsory education for children aged 5 to 16 years.
  • Article 27: Safeguard against discrimination in services.

Test trap: Fundamental rights can be enforced by the High Courts (Article 199) and the Supreme Court (Article 184).

Famous Articles — Jo Har Test Mein Aate Hain

Article 1: Pakistan is a Federal Republic, called the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

Article 2: Islam is the state religion of Pakistan.

Article 2-A: The Objectives Resolution is part of the Constitution.

Article 6: High treason — abrogating or suspending the Constitution is treason.

Article 41: The President is the head of state, elected for 5 years.

Articles 62 & 63: Qualifications and disqualifications of members of Parliament.

Article 140-A: Local governments — each province must set up a local government system.

Article 251: Urdu is the national language of Pakistan.

Important Amendments

  • 8th Amendment (1985): Gave the President power to dissolve the National Assembly (Article 58-2(b)) — later removed by the 13th Amendment.
  • 13th Amendment (1997): Removed the President's power to dissolve the Assembly.
  • 17th Amendment (2003): Partly restored that power.
  • 18th Amendment (2010): The biggest reform — provincial autonomy, the concurrent list was abolished and many powers went to the provinces; the name of NWFP was changed to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • 25th Amendment (2018): Merged FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • 26th Amendment (2024): Changed the way the Chief Justice and senior judges are appointed.

Quick Facts — Test Mein Aane Wale Points

  • The Constitution was framed by a committee headed by Mahmud Ali Kasuri (drafting committee) under the National Assembly.
  • The Objectives Resolution was passed on 12 March 1949 and later became Article 2-A.
  • Pakistan's first Constitution (1956) was abrogated in 1958; the second (1962) ended in 1969.
  • The President is elected by an electoral college: the Senate, the National Assembly and the four provincial assemblies.
  • The Prime Minister is elected by the National Assembly and must command its majority.
  • The Supreme Court is the highest court; its seat is in Islamabad.

    (a) 250   (b) 280   (c) 300   (d) 320

    (a) Prime Minister   (b) President   (c) Chief Justice   (d) Chairman Senate
  4. Fundamental rights are given in the Constitution in Articles:
    (a) 1 to 7   (b) 8 to 28   (c) 29 to 40   (d) 41 to 50
  5. The total number of seats in the National Assembly is:
    (a) 342   (b) 336   (c) 272   (d) 300
  6. The total number of seats in the Senate is:
    (a) 100   (b) 104   (c) 96   (d) 88
  7. Which amendment gave provincial autonomy in 2010?
    (a) 17th Amendment   (b) 18th Amendment   (c) 19th Amendment   (d) 21st Amendment

    (a) 1   (b) 2   (c) 3   (d) 9
  9. Urdu is the national language of Pakistan under Article:
    (a) 245   (b) 248   (c) 251   (d) 260
  10. The term of a Senator is:
    (a) 4 years   (b) 5 years   (c) 6 years   (d) 3 years
