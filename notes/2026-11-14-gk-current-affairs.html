GK & Current Affairs — Pakistan and International Organizations

General Knowledge International Organizations 10 Practice MCQs

The world organizations Pakistan belongs to — the UN, OIC, SAARC, ECO, SCO, Commonwealth, IMF, World Bank and ADB — their founding years, headquarters and Pakistan's membership, in short simple notes with Roman Urdu explanations, then 10 practice MCQs with an answer key.

United Nations (UN) — Aqwam-e-Muttahida

  • The United Nations was founded on 24 October 1945, after the Second World War, to keep peace in the world. Its headquarters is in New York, USA. Roman Urdu: Aqwam-e-Muttahida duniya mein aman qayam rakhne ke liye banayi gayi.
  • The UN has 193 member states. Pakistan joined the UN on 30 September 1947 — just six weeks after independence.
  • The Security Council is the most powerful UN body. It has 5 permanent members — the USA, UK, France, Russia and China — each with veto power, plus 10 non-permanent members elected for two years.
  • The General Assembly includes all 193 members; every country has one vote. The UN Secretary-General is its chief officer.

Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC)

  • The OIC was founded on 25 September 1969 in Rabat, Morocco. Its headquarters is in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. Pakistan is a founding member.
  • It has 57 member states — the second-largest intergovernmental organization in the world after the UN. Roman Urdu: yeh Musalman mumalik ki sab se bari tanzeem hai.
  • Its aim is to protect the interests of the Muslim world and to promote cooperation among Muslim countries.

SAARC — South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation

  • SAARC was founded on 8 December 1985 in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Its headquarters (Secretariat) is in Kathmandu, Nepal.
  • It has 8 member countries: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Maldives and Afghanistan (Afghanistan joined in 2007).
  • Its aim is regional cooperation in South Asia — trade, education, health and poverty reduction. Roman Urdu: SAARC ka maqsad Janubi Asia ke mumalik ke darmiyan taawun barhana hai.

ECO — Economic Cooperation Organization

  • The ECO was founded in 1985 and its headquarters is in Tehran, Iran. It has 10 member states.
  • Pakistan, Iran and Turkey are its founding members. The three countries first worked together as the RCD (Regional Cooperation for Development) in 1964; the ECO replaced the RCD in 1985.
  • Its aim is economic cooperation — trade, transport and energy — among its members.

SCO — Shanghai Cooperation Organisation

  • The SCO was founded on 15 June 2001 in Shanghai, China. Its headquarters is in Beijing, China.
  • Pakistan became a full member in 2017 (at the Astana summit). India joined as a full member in the same year.
  • Its aims are security, counter-terrorism and economic cooperation in Central and South Asia. Roman Urdu: SCO ka maqsad khatay mein aman, salamati aur maashi taawun hai.

The Commonwealth

  • The Commonwealth is a group of 56 countries, mostly former parts of the British Empire. Its headquarters is at Marlborough House, London.
  • Pakistan joined the Commonwealth at independence in 1947, left it in 1972, and rejoined in 1989.
  • Its head is the British monarch; its day-to-day work is led by the Commonwealth Secretary-General.

IMF & World Bank — Money Organizations

  • Both were created at the Bretton Woods Conference in 1944. Both have their headquarters in Washington DC, USA. Pakistan joined both in 1950.
  • The International Monetary Fund (IMF) lends money to countries facing balance-of-payments problems and gives economic advice. Roman Urdu: IMF un mumalik ko qarz deta hai jin ke paas dollars ki kami ho.
  • The World Bank lends money for development projects — roads, dams, schools and health — mainly to poor and developing countries.

Asian Development Bank (ADB)

  • The ADB was founded on 19 December 1966. Its headquarters is in Manila, Philippines. Pakistan is a founding member.
  • It lends money for development projects in Asia and the Pacific — transport, energy, water and education.

Quick Revision — Founding Year + Headquarters

  • UN — 1945, New York · OIC — 1969, Jeddah · SAARC — 1985, Kathmandu
  • ECO — 1985, Tehran · SCO — 2001, Beijing · Commonwealth — HQ London
  • IMF — 1944, Washington DC · World Bank — 1944, Washington DC · ADB — 1966, Manila
  • Pakistan joined: UN 1947 · Commonwealth 1947 (left 1972, rejoined 1989) · IMF & World Bank 1950 · SCO full member 2017

Common Test Traps

  • SAARC was founded in Dhaka but its headquarters is in Kathmandu — do not mix the two cities.
  • OIC headquarters is Jeddah, not Makkah or Riyadh.
  • UN founding year is 1945; OIC is 1969; SAARC and ECO are both 1985; SCO is 2001.
  • Pakistan joined the SCO as a full member in 2017, not in 2001 when the SCO was founded.
  • IMF and World Bank both sit in Washington DC; the UN sits in New York; the Commonwealth sits in London.
