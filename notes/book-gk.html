This small book covers the General Knowledge facts that come again and again in SPSC, FPSC, PST, JEST and other screening tests. Read the short notes first, learn the lists, and then try the 15 practice MCQs at the end.

World Capitals and Currencies

Questions on capitals and currencies are asked in almost every test. Learn these common pairs:

  • Pakistan — Capital: Islamabad · Currency: Rupee
  • India — Capital: New Delhi · Currency: Rupee
  • China — Capital: Beijing · Currency: Yuan (Renminbi)
  • Japan — Capital: Tokyo · Currency: Yen
  • United Kingdom — Capital: London · Currency: Pound Sterling
  • United States — Capital: Washington, D.C. · Currency: Dollar
  • France — Capital: Paris · Currency: Euro
  • Germany — Capital: Berlin · Currency: Euro
  • Saudi Arabia — Capital: Riyadh · Currency: Riyal
  • United Arab Emirates — Capital: Abu Dhabi · Currency: Dirham
  • Iran — Capital: Tehran · Currency: Rial
  • Türkiye — Capital: Ankara · Currency: Lira
  • Bangladesh — Capital: Dhaka · Currency: Taka
  • Australia — Capital: Canberra · Currency: Australian Dollar
  • Canada — Capital: Ottawa · Currency: Canadian Dollar

Trick to remember: the capital of Australia is Canberra, not Sydney, and the capital of Türkiye is Ankara, not Istanbul. Test makers love these two traps.

International Organisations

United Nations (UN) — Founded in 1945 after the Second World War. Headquarters: New York, USA. It has 193 member states. Its main aims are world peace, security and cooperation. Important UN bodies: UNESCO (Paris — education and culture), WHO (Geneva — health), UNICEF (New York — children), FAO (Rome — food and agriculture), IMF and World Bank (Washington, D.C. — money and development). United Nations Day is marked on 24 October.

OIC (Organisation of Islamic Cooperation) — Founded in 1969. Headquarters: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. It is the organisation of Muslim-majority countries and has 57 member states.

SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) — Founded in 1985. Headquarters: Kathmandu, Nepal. It has 8 members: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives and Afghanistan.

Pakistan General Knowledge

  • Founder of Pakistan: Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah — first Governor-General of Pakistan.
  • First Prime Minister: Liaquat Ali Khan. First President: Iskander Mirza.
  • Pakistan came into being on 14 August 1947.
  • First Constitution: 1956. Present Constitution: 1973.
  • National language: Urdu · National poet: Allama Muhammad Iqbal · National anthem writer: Hafeez Jalandhari.
  • National animal: Markhor · National bird: Chakor · National flower: Jasmine · National game: Hockey.
  • Largest province by area: Balochistan · Largest province by population: Punjab · Largest city: Karachi.
  • Longest river: the Indus River. Highest peak of Pakistan: K2 (8,611 metres), the second highest mountain in the world.
  • First woman Prime Minister of Pakistan (and of the Muslim world): Benazir Bhutto.

Science GK One-Liners

  • Chemical formula of water is H2O. The Sun is a star.
  • The gas we breathe in to live is oxygen; plants take in carbon dioxide for photosynthesis.
  • The nearest planet to the Sun is Mercury; the largest planet is Jupiter. We live in the Milky Way galaxy.
  • The normal temperature of a healthy human body is about 37°C (98.6°F).
  • Speed of light is about 300,000 km per second. The unit of electric current is the ampere.
  • The hardest natural substance on Earth is diamond. The most abundant gas in the air is nitrogen (about 78%).

Sports and Awards Basics

  • The Olympic Games are held every 4 years. The five Olympic rings stand for the inhabited continents.
  • Brazil has won the FIFA Football World Cup the most times (5 titles).
  • The Nobel Prizes were started in 1901. They are given for Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, Peace and Economics.
  • Pakistan's Malala Yousafzai is the youngest Nobel Peace Prize winner in history.
  • The Oscars are the famous awards for films, given in the United States.
  • Pakistan won the Cricket World Cup in 1992 under captain Imran Khan.
