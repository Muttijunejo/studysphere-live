Vibe Coding Roadmap 2027

A book by MR Academy · Author: Mutti Junejo · 104 pages · Simple English

About this book

This book teaches a new way of building software that has appeared in the AI era: you describe what you want in plain words, and AI tools help you turn that idea into a real, working app. People call this style vibe coding. You do not need years of study before you build your first project — you need a clear roadmap, the right tools, and the habit of practising a little every day. That is exactly what this book gives you.

What is inside — 21 chapters

  • What vibe coding really is, and the right mindset for working with AI
  • The 2027 toolbox: chat AI tools, AI code editors, app builders
  • Prompting like a pro, and the minimum tech you must understand
  • The roadmap: four phases to your first app, step by step
  • Logins, databases and real app features — plus debugging with AI
  • Ten practice projects and three fully guided projects
  • Publishing your work, and going from student to earner
  • A complete 90-day action plan, week by week
  • Appendix: 30 ready-to-use prompts, glossary and FAQ

Who is it for?

Students and beginners who want to build real apps and websites with AI tools — especially CS students. If you can type an idea in simple words, this book shows you the full path from idea to a working, published project.
