Topic 1: Exponents, Roots & Simplification — Short Notes

  • Exponent (power): a^n means a multiplied by itself n times. Example: 2^5 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32. The number a is the base and n is the exponent. Upar wali chhoti sankhya power hoti hai.
  • Rules of exponents: a^m × a^n = a^m+n  |  a^m ÷ a^n = a^m−n  |  (a^m)^n = a^mn  |  a^0 = 1  |  a^−n = 1/a^n.
  • Square root (√): √25 = 5 because 5 × 5 = 25. Cube root (∛): ∛27 = 3 because 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. Square root woh number hai jo khud se multiply karne par asal number de.
  • Perfect squares to remember: 1^2=1, 2^2=4, 3^2=9, 4^2=16, 5^2=25, 6^2=36, 7^2=49, 8^2=64, 9^2=81, 10^2=100, 11^2=121, 12^2=144, 13^2=169, 14^2=196, 15^2=225.
  • Simplification order (BODMAS / DMAS): first Brackets, then Division and Multiplication (left to right), then Addition and Subtraction (left to right). Example: 6 + 3 × 4 = 6 + 12 = 18 (not 36).
  • Useful identities: (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2  |  (a − b)^2 = a^2 − 2ab + b^2  |  a^2 − b^2 = (a + b)(a − b).
