Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Profit & Loss: Profit% hamesha Cost Price par nikalte hain — Profit% = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100. SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) ÷ 100.
  • Simple Interest: SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100; Amount = P + SI. Time hamesha saal mein ho — 9 months = 0.75 year. Agar raqam dugni ho jaye to Rate = 100 ÷ Time.
  • Islamiat: Khalilullah = Hazrat Ibrahim (AS), Kalimullah = Hazrat Musa (AS), Ruhullah = Hazrat Isa (AS), Zabihullah = Hazrat Ismail (AS); Zakat 2 AH mein farz hui; Quran mein “Ahmad” naam 1 dafa aaya hai.
  • Pakistan: teesre Governor-General — Malik Ghulam Muhammad; pehle nuclear tests 1998 mein huay; qomi janwar — Markhor; dusri buland tareen choti — Nanga Parbat.
  • Computer: CPU = Central Processing Unit; RAM = Random Access Memory; 1 MB = 1024 KB; Ctrl+A = select all, Ctrl+P = print; Monitor output device hai, Keyboard input device hai.
