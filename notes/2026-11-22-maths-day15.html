Topic 1: Fractions & Decimals — Short Notes

  • A fraction is a part of a whole. In 3/4, the top number (3) is the numerator and the bottom number (4) is the denominator. Upar wala numerator, neeche wala denominator kehlata hai.
  • Comparing fractions: cross-multiply. For 3/5 and 2/3: 3 × 3 = 9 and 2 × 5 = 10, so 2/3 is bigger. Cross multiply kar ke badi fraction pehchanein.
  • Simplify: divide top and bottom by their HCF. Example: 15/25 ÷ 5 = 3/5. Equivalent fractions: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6.
  • Add / subtract: same denominator — add only the numerators. Different denominators — first make them equal with the LCM. 1/4 + 2/3 = 3/12 + 8/12 = 11/12.
  • Multiply: numerator × numerator, denominator × denominator. 3/7 × 21 = 3 × 3 = 9. Divide: multiply by the reciprocal (second fraction ulta kar ke). 5/6 ÷ 2/3 = 5/6 × 3/2 = 5/4.
  • Decimals — place value: 0.05 = 5 hundredths = 1/20; 0.45 = 45 hundredths = 9/20. Decimal × decimal: multiply as whole numbers, then count the decimal places — 0.7 × 0.3 = 0.21.
  • Fraction ↔ decimal: divide numerator by denominator (7/20 = 0.35). Decimal → fraction: put over 10, 100 or 1000 and simplify (0.8 = 8/10 = 4/5).

Topic 2: Percentages — Short Notes

  • Percent means “out of 100”. 40% = 40/100 = 2/5. Percent ka matlab hai “100 mein se”.
  • % → decimal: divide by 100 (28% = 0.28). Decimal → %: multiply by 100 (0.28 = 28%). Fraction → %: multiply by 100 (4/25 = 16%).
  • % of a number: (percent × number) ÷ 100. 40% of 250 = 40 × 250 ÷ 100 = 100.
  • Increase: new value = old × (1 + %/100). 150 increased by 20% = 150 × 1.20 = 180. Decrease: new value = old × (1 − %/100). 500 decreased by 8% = 500 × 0.92 = 460.
  • Finding the %: (part ÷ whole) × 100. 45 out of 180 = 45/180 × 100 = 25%.
  • Finding the whole: if 30% of a number is 90, the number = 90 ÷ 30 × 100 = 300.
  • Two percents in a row: 25% of 25% of 400 — first 25% of 400 = 100, then 25% of 100 = 25.
