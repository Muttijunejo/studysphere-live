Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Maths: ratio simplify karne ke liye dono hisso ko un ke HCF se taqseem karein — 45 : 60 = 3 : 4; Average = sum ÷ count; pehle 10 natural numbers ka average = 5.5.
  • Revision (Kaizen): 5/6 of 240 = 200; 25% of 480 = 120; A 10 din + B 15 din = aik saath 6 din; 108 km/h = 30 m/s; 34 = 81.
  • Islamiat: Quran ki aakhri Surah — Surah An-Nas; sab se chhoti Surah — Surah Al-Kawthar; pehle Khalifa — Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA); Ghazwa-e-Khandaq 5 AH mein lari gayi.
  • Pakistan: pehle Foreign Minister — Zafarullah Khan; national bird — Chukar partridge; raqbe ke lehaz se sab se chhota suba — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; pehla martial law 1958 mein laga.
  • Computer: CPU ko computer ka “brain” kehte hain; copy karne ke liye Ctrl+C; 1 byte = 8 bits; ATM = Automated Teller Machine.
