Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Profit & Loss: Profit% hamesha Cost Price par nikalte hain — Profit% = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100. SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) ÷ 100.
  • Simple Interest: SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100; Amount = P + SI. Time hamesha saal mein ho — 6 months = 0.5 year.
  • Pakistan: Quaid-e-Azam died on 11 September 1948; Khawaja Nazimuddin was the second Governor-General; Balochistan is the largest province by area; Mangla Dam is on the Jhelum River.
  • Sindhi: 1 = Hik ھڪ, 2 = Ba ٻہ; Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai is Sindh's famous Sufi poet; Urdu is written in Nastaliq script.
