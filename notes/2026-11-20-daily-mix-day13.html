Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Number System: sab se chhota odd composite number 9 hai; HCF(56, 84) = 28; do numbers ka product = HCF × LCM; pehle 20 natural numbers ka sum = 210.
  • Sets & Statistics: n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B); 5 elements wale set ke 32 subsets hote hain (2^5); pehle 8 natural numbers ka mean = 4.5; median = darmiyan wali value, mode = sab se zyada aane wali value.
  • Islamiat: Quran mein 30 Paras (Juz) hain; Surah At-Taubah Bismillah se shuru nahi hoti; Surah Al-Fatiha har Rakat mein parhi jati hai; Eid-ul-Adha Zil-Hajj mein hoti hai.
  • Sindhi: cat = ٻلي, fish = مڇي; five = پنج, ten = ڏهه; teacher = استاد, friend = دوست, red = ڳاڙهو, hair = وار.
