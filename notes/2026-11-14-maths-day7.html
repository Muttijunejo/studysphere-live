Topic 1: Exponents, Roots & Simplification — Short Notes

  • Exponent (power): an means a multiplied by itself n times. Example: 25 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32. The number a is the base and n is the exponent. Upar wali chhoti sankhya power hoti hai.
  • Rules of exponents: am × an = am+n  |  am ÷ an = am−n  |  (am)n = amn  |  a0 = 1  |  a−n = 1/an.
  • Square root (√): √25 = 5 because 5 × 5 = 25. Cube root (∛): ∛27 = 3 because 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. Square root woh number hai jo khud se multiply karne par asal number de.
  • Perfect squares to remember: 12=1, 22=4, 32=9, 42=16, 52=25, 62=36, 72=49, 82=64, 92=81, 102=100, 112=121, 122=144, 132=169, 142=196, 152=225.
  • Simplification order (BODMAS / DMAS): first Brackets, then Division and Multiplication (left to right), then Addition and Subtraction (left to right). Example: 6 + 3 × 4 = 6 + 12 = 18 (not 36).
  • Useful identities: (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2  |  (a − b)2 = a2 − 2ab + b2  |  a2 − b2 = (a + b)(a − b).
