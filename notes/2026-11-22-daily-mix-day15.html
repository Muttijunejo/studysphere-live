Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Maths: 3/8 of 400 = 150; 35% of 300 = 105; 7/8 ÷ 1/4 = 7/2; 0.9 × 0.9 = 0.81; percent nikalne ke liye (part ÷ whole) × 100.
  • Revision (Kaizen): 2/3 of 240 = 160; 45% of 400 = 180; A 15 din + B 10 din = aik saath 6 din; 90 km/h = 25 m/s; pehle 8 natural numbers ka average = 4.5.
  • Islamiat: Surah Yaseen “Heart of the Quran” kehlati hai; pehli Madani Surah — Surah Al-Baqarah; Sulah Hudaybiyyah 6 AH mein hui; “Muhammad” naam Quran mein 4 martaba aaya; pehle muezzin — Hazrat Bilal (RA).
  • Pakistan: Mangla Dam Jhelum par hai; national anthem 1954 mein adopt hua; national tree — Deodar; K2 Gilgit-Baltistan mein hai; Sialkot sports goods ke liye mashhoor hai.
  • Sindhi: Mother = Ama (امّا), Brother = Bhaao (ڀاءُ), Star = Taro (تارو), Sun = Sij (سج), One = Hik (هڪ), House = Ghar (گھر).
