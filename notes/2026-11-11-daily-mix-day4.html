Important Points — Aaj Ke Khaas Notes

  • Time & Work: Aik din ka kaam = 1 ÷ din. Dono mil kar = 1/x + 1/y; din = (x × y) ÷ (x + y). Zyada aadmi hon to kam din lagte hain.
  • Time, Speed & Distance: Speed = Distance ÷ Time. km/h → m/s: 5/18 se zarb do. Aik hi fasla dono taraf: Average speed = 2xy ÷ (x + y).
  • Pakistan: First President — Iskander Mirza (1956); 1973 Constitution 14 August 1973 ko nafiz hua; national motto — Faith, Unity, Discipline; national flag Amiruddin Kidwai ne design kiya.
  • Computer: Ctrl+V = paste, Ctrl+S = save; documents ke liye MS Word, hisaab ke liye MS Excel; ROM = Read Only Memory.
