Important points before you apply

  • ASF is a uniformed force — a physical test (running, height and chest measurement) is part of selection. Minimum height for male candidates is 5'-6".
  • Registration needs your CNIC number, CNIC expiry date and a recent photograph.
  • Apply only on the official website joinasf.gov.pk. Never pay any agent — registration is free.
  • Posting can be at any airport in Pakistan.

Is ASF ASI a good post for Intermediate students?

Yes. ASI is BPS-11, needs only Intermediate, and is the highest-scale uniformed post in this advertisement open at Intermediate level.
