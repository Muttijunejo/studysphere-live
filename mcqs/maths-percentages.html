Percentage MCQs (Maths)

By MR Academy — Published 2026-11-08 — Last updated 2026-11-08

These are original Practice MCQs on Percentages — finding a percent of a number, percentage increase and decrease, marks and result percentages, discount, and profit and loss — for PST, JEST, SST, SPSC and other test preparation. Every answer carries a short worked explanation so you can learn the method. They are practice material written by MR Academy, not official past-paper questions. Click an option to select it, then press Show Answers to see the correct answers, explanations and your score.

1. 50% of 200 is:

Answer: A. 50% means half: 200 x 50/100 = 100.

2. 25% of 80 is:

Answer: D. 25% is one quarter: 80 x 25/100 = 20.

3. 10% of 450 is:

Answer: B. 10% is one tenth: 450 / 10 = 45.

4. 75% of 64 is:

Answer: B. 75% = 3/4, and 64 x 3/4 = 48.

5. 20 is what percent of 50?

Answer: D. 20/50 x 100 = 40%.

6. 15 out of 60, expressed as a percentage, is:

Answer: A. 15/60 = 1/4, and 1/4 x 100 = 25%.

7. 5% of 1000 is:

Answer: C. 1000 x 5/100 = 50.

8. 12.5% of 160 is:

Answer: D. 12.5% is 1/8 of a number: 160 / 8 = 20.

9. 33 1/3% of 90 is:

Answer: C. 33 1/3% means one third: 90 / 3 = 30.

10. Increase 80 by 25%:

Answer: B. 25% of 80 is 20, and 80 + 20 = 100.

11. Decrease 120 by 10%:

Answer: A. 10% of 120 is 12, and 120 - 12 = 108.

12. A number rises from 40 to 50. The percentage increase is:

Answer: A. Increase = 10; 10/40 x 100 = 25%. Percentage change is always found on the original number.

13. A shirt costs Rs 500. With a 20% discount, you pay:

Answer: C. Discount = 20% of 500 = Rs 100, so 500 - 100 = Rs 400.

14. A salary of Rs 40,000 is increased by 10%. The new salary is:

Answer: B. 10% of 40,000 = 4,000, and 40,000 + 4,000 = Rs 44,000.

15. 90% of 300 is:

Answer: D. 300 x 90/100 = 270.

16. If 30% of a number is 45, the number is:

Answer: B. The number = 45 x 100/30 = 150.

17. If 25% of x is 30, then x equals:

Answer: A. x = 30 x 100/25 = 120.

18. A student scores 45 marks out of 50. His percentage is:

Answer: D. 45/50 x 100 = 90%.

19. 18 marks out of 24 is equal to:

Answer: C. 18/24 = 3/4, and 3/4 x 100 = 75%.

20. 36 out of 120, as a percentage, is:

Answer: C. 36/120 x 100 = 30%.

21. In a class of 200 students, 60% are girls. The number of girls is:

Answer: D. 200 x 60/100 = 120 girls.

22. Out of 150 mangoes, 20% are rotten. How many mangoes are rotten?

Answer: A. 150 x 20/100 = 30.

23. An article bought for Rs 800 is sold for Rs 1000. The profit percent is:

Answer: B. Profit = Rs 200; profit % is found on cost price: 200/800 x 100 = 25%.

24. A cycle bought for Rs 500 is sold for Rs 450. The loss percent is:

Answer: D. Loss = Rs 50; 50/500 x 100 = 10% loss.

25. 1% of 2500 is:

Answer: B. 1% means 1 out of every 100: 2500 / 100 = 25.

26. 60% of 60 is:

Answer: C. 60 x 60/100 = 36.

27. What percent of 200 is 50?

Answer: A. 50/200 x 100 = 25%.

28. 0.45 expressed as a percentage is:

Answer: A. Multiply a decimal by 100 to get percent: 0.45 x 100 = 45%.

29. 3/5 expressed as a percentage is:

Answer: D. 3/5 = 0.6, and 0.6 x 100 = 60%.

30. 35% expressed as a fraction in its simplest form is:

Answer: B. 35% = 35/100; dividing top and bottom by 5 gives 7/20.

31. Increase 250 by 8%:

Answer: C. 8% of 250 is 20, and 250 + 20 = 270.

32. Decrease 90 by 30%:

Answer: B. 30% of 90 is 27, and 90 - 27 = 63.

33. After a 20% discount, a bag sells for Rs 80. Its original price was:

Answer: C. Rs 80 is 80% of the original price, so the original = 80 x 100/80 = Rs 100.

34. A town's population of 10,000 grows by 5% in a year. The new population is:

Answer: D. 5% of 10,000 = 500, and 10,000 + 500 = 10,500.

35. 40% of 150 is:

Answer: A. 150 x 40/100 = 60.

36. 85% of the 200 total marks of a test is:

Answer: A. 200 x 85/100 = 170 marks.

37. A jacket marked Rs 1200 is sold at a 25% discount. The sale price is:

Answer: B. Discount = 25% of 1200 = Rs 300, so 1200 - 300 = Rs 900.

38. 15% of 200 is:

Answer: C. 200 x 15/100 = 30.

39. 60 is what percent of 240?

Answer: D. 60/240 x 100 = 25%.

40. In an election, a candidate gets 60% of 2500 votes. How many votes did he get?

Answer: C. 2500 x 60/100 = 1500 votes.
