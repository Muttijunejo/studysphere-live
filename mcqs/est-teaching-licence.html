EST — Teaching Licence MCQs

By MR Academy — Published 2026-11-08 — Last updated 2026-11-08

These are original Practice MCQs for the Elementary School Teacher (EST) post and the Sindh Teaching Licence — covering STEDA and the 2023 licence policy basics, the stages of schooling, Article 25-A, and classroom assessment in practice. They are practice material written by MR Academy from well-settled facts; they are NOT official STEDA or IBA paper questions, and the syllabus and dates must always be confirmed from the official advertisement. Click an option to select it, then press Show Answers to see the correct answers, explanations and your score.

1. EST stands for:

Answer: C. EST = Elementary School Teacher, the teacher appointed for the elementary (middle) stage of schooling.

2. STEDA stands for:

Answer: C. STEDA is the Sindh Teacher Education Development Authority, the body for teacher education and licensing in Sindh.

3. In Sindh, the authority responsible for teacher education and the teaching licence is:

Answer: D. STEDA looks after teacher education, professional standards and the teaching licence in Sindh.

4. The Sindh Teaching Licence Policy was approved in:

Answer: A. The Government of Sindh approved its Teaching Licence Policy in 2023, making licensing part of teacher recruitment.

5. EST is a post of grade:

Answer: B. The Elementary School Teacher (EST) post is in BPS-16 — above the BPS-14 PST and JEST posts.

6. EST is different from PST and JEST because EST is:

Answer: D. EST was created as a separate BPS-16 post under the licence policy; it did not simply rename the older PST/JEST posts.

7. In Pakistan's school system, the elementary stage covers classes:

Answer: B. Primary is classes 1–5, elementary (middle) is classes 6–8, and secondary is classes 9–10.

8. An Elementary School Teacher (EST) generally teaches classes:

Answer: C. ESTs are appointed for the elementary stage — classes six, seven and eight.

9. The primary stage of schooling covers classes:

Answer: A. Classes 1–5 form the primary stage, taught mainly by Primary School Teachers (PST).

10. PST stands for:

Answer: A. PST = Primary School Teacher, the BPS-14 teacher of the primary classes.

11. JEST stands for:

Answer: D. JEST = Junior Elementary School Teacher, a BPS-14 post of the Sindh Education Department.

12. The secondary stage of schooling covers classes:

Answer: B. After the elementary stage (6–8), students enter the secondary stage — classes 9 and 10, ending with the matriculation exam.

13. Article 25-A of the Constitution of Pakistan gives every child the right to:

Answer: C. Article 25-A makes free and compulsory education a fundamental right of every child.

14. Under Article 25-A, free and compulsory education is the right of children aged:

Answer: B. The Constitution fixes the age band of 5 to 16 years for free and compulsory education.

15. Article 25-A was added to the Constitution through the:

Answer: C. The 18th Amendment (2010) inserted Article 25-A and also made education a provincial subject.

16. The department that runs government schools in Sindh is the:

Answer: D. Government schooling in Sindh is administered by the School Education and Literacy Department (SELD).

17. Recruitment tests for Sindh's government teacher posts (PST, JEST, EST) are conducted through:

Answer: A. Sukkur IBA Testing Service (STS) conducts the screening tests for Sindh teacher recruitment.

18. The textbooks used in Sindh's government schools are published by the:

Answer: A. The Sindh Textbook Board (STBB), Jamshoro, publishes the prescribed textbooks for Sindh schools.

19. BISE stands for:

Answer: B. The BISE boards conduct the matric (secondary) and intermediate examinations.

20. The matriculation examination is taken at the end of class:

Answer: C. Matriculation (SSC) completes the secondary stage at the end of class 10.

21. ECE, the pre-primary class in schools, stands for:

Answer: D. ECE is the early-childhood (katchi) stage that prepares young children for class 1.

22. The main purpose of a teaching licence is to:

Answer: C. Licensing, like in medicine or law, checks that a person is properly qualified and competent to teach.

23. A teaching-licence test mainly checks a candidate's:

Answer: A. The licence test covers what the teacher will teach (subject) and how to teach it (pedagogy).

24. Formative assessment is carried out:

Answer: D. Formative assessment — class questions, short quizzes, observation — is done during learning so teaching can be improved in time.

25. Summative assessment is generally held:

Answer: B. Summative assessment, such as the annual examination, measures the total learning at the end of a period.

26. Diagnostic assessment is used to find out:

Answer: B. A diagnostic test pinpoints where a learner is stuck, so the teacher can plan remedial help.

27. In Bloom's taxonomy, the lowest level of learning is:

Answer: D. The revised taxonomy starts with Remembering — recalling facts — and rises to Creating at the top.

28. SLOs, written in lesson plans and textbooks, stand for:

Answer: A. An SLO states what the student will be able to do after learning a topic.

29. The first and most important part to decide in a lesson plan is:

Answer: C. Objectives come first: activities, questions and tests are all chosen to achieve them.

30. A test that measures exactly what it was made to measure is called:

Answer: D. Validity means the test truly measures the intended learning outcomes.

31. A test is reliable when it:

Answer: C. Reliability is consistency: the same learner scores nearly the same on a repeat under similar conditions.

32. Continuous assessment means assessment that is:

Answer: B. Instead of judging by one final paper, continuous assessment records learning throughout the year.

33. The table of specifications (blueprint) of a test paper shows:

Answer: A. A blueprint keeps the paper balanced across topics, difficulty levels and question types before it is set.

34. Licensing of teachers is a step towards treating teaching as:

Answer: A. Like doctors and engineers, teachers are licensed so that teaching is recognised and practised as a profession.

35. The Single National Curriculum (SNC) was introduced in Pakistan to:

Answer: C. The SNC aimed at one uniform curriculum for government, private and madrassa streams alike.

36. DEO, the district-level education officer, stands for:

Answer: B. The District Education Officer supervises government schools and teachers at district level.

37. A head teacher's main academic duty in a school is to:

Answer: D. The head teacher is the academic leader: observing classes, guiding teachers and checking learning.

38. Asking questions during a lesson mainly helps the teacher to:

Answer: B. Questioning gives instant feedback on learning and keeps students thinking.

39. Micro-teaching is a training technique in which a trainee:

Answer: A. In micro-teaching one skill (e.g. questioning) is practised in a few minutes with a few learners, followed by feedback.

40. In classroom assessment, a portfolio is:

Answer: D. Portfolios store samples of a learner's work across weeks, showing progress better than one test alone.
