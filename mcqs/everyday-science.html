Everyday Science MCQs

By MR Academy — Published 2026-11-08 — Last updated 2026-11-08

These are original Practice MCQs on Everyday Science (General Science) — the human body, heat, light and sound, plants, simple units and instruments, and the chemistry of daily life — for PST, JEST, SST, SPSC and other test preparation. All facts are stable textbook facts. They are practice material written by MR Academy, not official past-paper questions. Click an option to select it, then press Show Answers to see the correct answers, explanations and your score.

1. The SI unit of temperature is:

Answer: C. In the SI system temperature is measured in kelvin (K); Celsius is the common everyday scale.

2. At sea level, pure water boils at:

Answer: D. At normal air pressure (sea level) water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

3. Pure water freezes (turns to ice) at:

Answer: C. On the Celsius scale the freezing point of water is 0 degrees, the fixed lower point of the scale.

4. The speed of light in a vacuum is about:

Answer: B. Light is the fastest thing known: about 3 x 10^8 metres per second, i.e. 300,000 km/s.

5. The SI unit of electric current is:

Answer: A. Current is measured in amperes (A); volt measures voltage and ohm measures resistance.

6. The SI unit of force is:

Answer: A. Force is measured in newtons (N), named after Sir Isaac Newton.

7. For photosynthesis, green plants take in:

Answer: C. Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the air and use it, with water and sunlight, to make food.

8. The gas released by green plants during photosynthesis is:

Answer: B. Photosynthesis splits water and releases oxygen as a by-product — the oxygen we breathe.

9. Burning (combustion) of any fuel needs:

Answer: D. No fire can burn without oxygen; that is why covering a flame puts it out.

10. The largest organ of the human body is:

Answer: B. The skin covers the whole body and is its largest organ, protecting it from germs and injury.

11. The normal temperature of a healthy human body is about:

Answer: A. Normal body temperature is about 37 degrees Celsius (98.6 degrees Fahrenheit).

12. An adult human body has how many bones?

Answer: D. Babies are born with about 300 bones, but many join together, leaving 206 in an adult.

13. The human heart has how many chambers?

Answer: C. The heart has four chambers: two atria on top and two ventricles below.

14. Which vitamin is made in our skin with the help of sunlight?

Answer: C. Sunlight on the skin helps the body make vitamin D, which keeps bones strong.

15. Anaemia is mainly caused by the deficiency of:

Answer: D. Iron is needed to make haemoglobin in red blood cells; its shortage causes anaemia.

16. Scurvy, a disease of the gums, is caused by the lack of:

Answer: A. Vitamin C (found in citrus fruits and amla) prevents scurvy.

17. The hardest substance in the human body is:

Answer: B. Enamel, the white outer layer of the teeth, is harder than bone.

18. The part of a plant that makes food is the:

Answer: D. Leaves contain chlorophyll and carry out photosynthesis, so they are the food factory of the plant.

19. The chemical formula of water is:

Answer: B. Each water molecule has two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom: H2O.

20. Common salt used in food is chemically:

Answer: C. Common salt is sodium chloride (NaCl), obtained from sea water and salt mines.

21. Lemons taste sour because they contain:

Answer: A. Citrus fruits contain citric acid; vinegar contains acetic acid.

22. Balloons that float in air are usually filled with:

Answer: A. Helium is much lighter than air and does not catch fire, so it is used in balloons.

23. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?

Answer: D. Mercury is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature; it is used in thermometers.

24. Electric wires in homes are commonly made of:

Answer: B. Copper conducts electricity very well and is not too costly, so it is used for wiring.

25. Sound cannot travel through:

Answer: C. Sound needs a material medium; in a vacuum there is nothing to carry the vibrations.

26. Sound travels fastest through:

Answer: B. Particles are closest in solids, so vibrations pass quickest: solids > liquids > gases.

27. A thermometer is used to measure:

Answer: C. Thermometers measure temperature; a barometer measures air pressure.

28. Atmospheric (air) pressure is measured by:

Answer: D. The barometer measures air pressure and helps in weather forecasting.

29. Objects fall towards the Earth because of:

Answer: A. Gravity is the Earth's pull on every object towards its centre.

30. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

Answer: A. Mars looks red because of the reddish iron-rich dust on its surface.

31. The planet nearest to the Sun is:

Answer: B. Mercury is the first and smallest planet, closest to the Sun.

32. The cells in blood that carry oxygen are:

Answer: C. Red blood cells contain haemoglobin, which carries oxygen to all parts of the body.

33. Digestion of food begins in the:

Answer: D. In the mouth, teeth cut the food and saliva starts changing starch into sugar.

34. A neutral solution, like pure water, has a pH of:

Answer: C. On the pH scale, 7 is neutral; values below 7 are acidic and above 7 are alkaline.

35. Which of these is a renewable source of energy?

Answer: A. Sunlight is renewable — it does not finish — while coal, petrol and gas are used up once burnt.

36. When water evaporates from the skin (as sweat), it produces:

Answer: D. Evaporation takes heat from the skin, which is why sweating cools the body.

37. The SI unit of length is:

Answer: B. The metre (m) is the SI unit of length; kilometre and centimetre are its multiples and parts.

38. The cells that protect the body by fighting germs are:

Answer: B. White blood cells are the body's soldiers; they attack and destroy germs.

39. The main source of energy for the Earth is:

Answer: D. Almost all energy on Earth — light, heat, wind and plant food — starts from the Sun.

40. Green plants are called producers because they:

Answer: A. Through photosynthesis plants produce their own food, and all other living things depend on them.
