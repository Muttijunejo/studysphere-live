English — Vocabulary & Grammar

1. Choose the synonym of 'Assist':

  • A. Hinder
  • B. Help
  • C. Delay
  • D. Avoid
Answer: B — Help. To assist means to help.

2. Choose the antonym of 'Urgent':

  • A. Immediate
  • B. Pressing
  • C. Unimportant
  • D. Sudden
Answer: C — Unimportant. Urgent means requiring quick action; unimportant is the opposite sense here.

3. The file was submitted ___ time.

  • A. in
  • B. on
  • C. at
  • D. by of
Answer: A — in. 'In time' means before the deadline.

4. Choose the correct spelling:

  • A. Seperately
  • B. Separately
  • C. Seprately
  • D. Separatelly
Answer: B — Separately. The correct spelling is 'separately'.

5. He deals ___ stationery.

  • A. with
  • B. in
  • C. of
  • D. for
Answer: B — in. We say 'deal in' goods (and 'deal with' people).

6. One word: 'A list of items to be discussed in a meeting' is an:

  • A. Agenda
  • B. Minutes
  • C. Circular
  • D. Memo
Answer: A — Agenda. An agenda lists the points for discussion.

7. The record of a meeting's decisions is called:

  • A. Agenda
  • B. Minutes
  • C. Notice
  • D. Report only
Answer: B — Minutes. Minutes record what was decided in a meeting.

8. Choose the synonym of 'Require':

  • A. Need
  • B. Give
  • C. Offer
  • D. Return
Answer: A — Need. To require means to need.

9. She typed the letter ___ the manager signed it.

  • A. because
  • B. before
  • C. so that of
  • D. unless of
Answer: B — before. 'Before' correctly orders the two actions.

10. The plural of 'File' in office use and the correct sentence is:

  • A. Files — The files are ready.
  • B. Filez — Files is ready.
  • C. Filen — The filen are ready.
  • D. Files — The files is ready.
Answer: A — Files — The files are ready.. Plural is 'files', and it takes a plural verb 'are'.

11. Choose the antonym of 'Accept':

  • A. Receive
  • B. Reject
  • C. Take
  • D. Admit
Answer: B — Reject. Accept's opposite is reject.

12. An official letter that goes to many offices is a:

  • A. Memo
  • B. Circular
  • C. Diary
  • D. Draft only
Answer: B — Circular. A circular is sent to many recipients.

13. He is responsible ___ maintaining the records.

  • A. of
  • B. for
  • C. to
  • D. with
Answer: B — for. We say 'responsible for' a duty.

14. Choose the correct sentence:

  • A. Please find attach the file.
  • B. Please find attached the file.
  • C. Please find attaching the file.
  • D. Please find attaches the file.
Answer: B — Please find attached the file.. 'Please find attached' is the correct formal phrase.

15. One word: 'To send a letter/document to a higher authority' is to:

  • A. Forward
  • B. Return
  • C. File
  • D. Copy
Answer: A — Forward. To forward means to send on to another person/office.

Verbal Reasoning

16. Pen is to Paper as Chalk is to:

  • A. Teacher
  • B. Blackboard
  • C. School
  • D. Write
Answer: B — Blackboard. Chalk is used on a blackboard, as a pen on paper.

17. Find the odd one out:

  • A. Rose
  • B. Jasmine
  • C. Lotus
  • D. Mango
Answer: D — Mango. Mango is a fruit; the others are flowers.

18. If OFFICE is coded as PGGJDF (each letter shifted +1), then CLERK is:

  • A. DMFLS
  • B. DMFSL
  • C. DMFLT
  • D. DNFLS
Answer: B — DMFSL. Shift each letter one step forward: C→D, L→M, E→F, R→S, K→L, giving DMFSL.

19. Doctor is to Patient as Clerk is to:

  • A. File
  • B. Public / Visitor
  • C. Office
  • D. Desk
Answer: B — Public / Visitor. A clerk serves/deals with the public or visitors, as a doctor treats patients.

20. Which word does not belong?

  • A. Table
  • B. Chair
  • C. Desk
  • D. Spoon
Answer: D — Spoon. Spoon is cutlery; the others are furniture.

21. Key is to Lock as Password is to:

  • A. Computer account
  • B. Keyboard
  • C. Screen
  • D. Letter
Answer: A — Computer account. A password 'opens' an account as a key opens a lock.

22. Find the odd one out (places):

  • A. Karachi
  • B. Lahore
  • C. Islamabad
  • D. Punjab
Answer: D — Punjab. Punjab is a province; the others are cities.

23. Winter is to Cold as Summer is to:

  • A. Rain
  • B. Heat
  • C. Wind
  • D. Season
Answer: B — Heat. Summer is known for heat, as winter for cold.

24. If CAT = 24 (3+1+20), then BAT = ?

  • A. 23
  • B. 24
  • C. 25
  • D. 21
Answer: A — 23. B=2, A=1, T=20 → 23.

25. Wheel is to Car as Page is to:

  • A. Book
  • B. Read
  • C. Paper only
  • D. Cover
Answer: A — Book. Pages are parts of a book, as wheels are parts of a car.

Non-Verbal / Analytical Reasoning

26. What comes next: 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, ___?

  • A. 25
  • B. 27
  • C. 29
  • D. 31
Answer: C — 29. These are prime numbers; the next prime is 29.

27. Complete the series: B, E, H, K, ___

  • A. M
  • B. N
  • C. O
  • D. L
Answer: B — N. Letters jump +3 each time: next is N.

28. In a puzzle, pairs follow the rule: result = (a + b)² − (a + b). Example: 2 and 3 give (5)² − 5 = 20. Using the same rule, 5 and 6 give:

  • A. 100
  • B. 110
  • C. 90
  • D. 121
Answer: B — 110. (5 + 6)² − (5 + 6) = 121 − 11 = 110.

29. A clerk files documents from 9:00 to 9:45, then from 10:15 to 11:00. Total filing time is:

  • A. 75 minutes
  • B. 90 minutes
  • C. 105 minutes
  • D. 120 minutes
Answer: B — 90 minutes. 45 + 45 = 90 minutes.

30. Find the missing number: 100, 90, 81, 73, 66, ___

  • A. 58
  • B. 59
  • C. 60
  • D. 61
Answer: C — 60. Differences decrease: −10, −9, −8, −7, so next −6 → 60.

31. If a man faces West and turns 90° clockwise, he now faces:

  • A. North
  • B. South
  • C. East
  • D. West
Answer: A — North. Clockwise from West: North.

32. Odd one out: 8, 27, 64, 100

  • A. 8
  • B. 27
  • C. 64
  • D. 100
Answer: D — 100. 8, 27, 64 are perfect cubes (2³, 3³, 4³); 100 is not.

33. The next letter pair: AB, DE, GH, JK, ___

  • A. LM
  • B. MN
  • C. NO
  • D. OP
Answer: B — MN. Pairs skip one letter between groups: after JK comes MN.

34. Raza is taller than Imran. Imran is taller than Bilal. So the shortest is:

  • A. Raza
  • B. Imran
  • C. Bilal
  • D. Cannot say
Answer: C — Bilal. Bilal is the shortest of the three.

35. What is the next number: 1, 8, 27, 64, ___?

  • A. 100
  • B. 125
  • C. 216
  • D. 81
Answer: B — 125. Cubes: 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³ → 5³ = 125.

General Knowledge

36. GHQ stands for:

  • A. General Headquarters
  • B. Government Headquarters
  • C. General Health Quarters
  • D. Guard Headquarters
Answer: A — General Headquarters. GHQ = General Headquarters (of the Pakistan Army).

37. The Pakistan Army's general headquarters is located in:

  • A. Karachi
  • B. Rawalpindi
  • C. Lahore
  • D. Islamabad
Answer: B — Rawalpindi. GHQ is in Rawalpindi.

38. Civilian staff in military offices in Pakistan are recruited through tests for posts like:

  • A. UDC and LDC (clerks)
  • B. Pilots
  • C. Surgeons
  • D. Judges
Answer: A — UDC and LDC (clerks). UDC (Upper Division Clerk) and LDC (Lower Division Clerk) are common civilian clerical posts.

39. The Pakistan Army was formally established in:

  • A. 1947
  • B. 1948
  • C. 1950
  • D. 1956
Answer: A — 1947. The Pakistan Army came into being at independence in 1947.

40. The first Commander-in-Chief of the Pakistan Army was:

  • A. General Ayub Khan
  • B. General Frank Messervy
  • C. General Douglas Gracey
  • D. General Tikka Khan
Answer: B — General Frank Messervy. General Sir Frank Messervy was the first C-in-C (1947).

41. The Pakistan Army's motto (Iman, Taqwa, Jihad-fi-Sabilillah) means:

  • A. Faith, Piety, Struggle in the path of Allah
  • B. Unity, Faith, Discipline
  • C. Courage and Honour
  • D. Service before Self
Answer: A — Faith, Piety, Struggle in the path of Allah. The Army's motto is Iman, Taqwa, Jihad-fi-Sabilillah.

42. Quaid-e-Azam's golden principles for the nation are:

  • A. Unity, Faith and Discipline
  • B. Work, Work and Work
  • C. Peace and Prosperity
  • D. Education for all
Answer: A — Unity, Faith and Discipline. Quaid-e-Azam's famous motto is 'Unity, Faith and Discipline'.

43. The Corps of Military Police is responsible for:

  • A. Discipline and security duties in the Army
  • B. Medical care
  • C. Signals
  • D. Engineering
Answer: A — Discipline and security duties in the Army. Military Police handle discipline, traffic and security duties.

44. Pakistan's Defence Day is observed on 6 September to remember the:

  • A. 1948 war
  • B. 1965 war
  • C. 1971 war
  • D. Kargil conflict
Answer: B — 1965 war. It commemorates the 1965 war.

45. The highest gallantry award of Pakistan is:

  • A. Sitara-e-Jurat
  • B. Nishan-e-Haider
  • C. Hilal-e-Jurat
  • D. Tamgha-e-Jurat
Answer: B — Nishan-e-Haider. Nishan-e-Haider is the highest gallantry award.

46. Major Raja Aziz Bhatti Shaheed received Nishan-e-Haider for the war of:

  • A. 1948
  • B. 1965
  • C. 1971
  • D. 1999
Answer: B — 1965. Major Aziz Bhatti was martyred defending Lahore in 1965.

47. The Pakistan Army Medical Corps' main teaching hospital in Rawalpindi is:

  • A. CMH Rawalpindi
  • B. PNS Shifa
  • C. PAF Hospital
  • D. Civil Hospital
Answer: A — CMH Rawalpindi. CMH (Combined Military Hospital) Rawalpindi is the flagship military hospital.

48. The Inter Services Public Relations (ISPR) is the media wing of:

  • A. Government only
  • B. The Armed Forces of Pakistan
  • C. Police
  • D. Judiciary
Answer: B — The Armed Forces of Pakistan. ISPR handles media/public relations for the armed forces.

49. Pakistan's military academy for the Navy (Naval Academy) is in:

  • A. Karachi
  • B. Gwadar
  • C. Ormara
  • D. Lahore
Answer: A — Karachi. The Pakistan Naval Academy is at Manora, Karachi.

50. The Air Force's primary academy (PAF Academy) is at:

  • A. Risalpur
  • B. Karachi
  • C. Sargodha
  • D. Peshawar
Answer: A — Risalpur. PAF Academy Asghar Khan is at Risalpur.

Pakistan Studies

51. The first Session of the All-India Muslim League was held at:

  • A. Dhaka
  • B. Karachi
  • C. Lahore
  • D. Aligarh
Answer: B — Karachi. After its founding at Dhaka (1906), the League's first session was held at Karachi in 1907.

52. The Nehru Report was published in:

  • A. 1927
  • B. 1928
  • C. 1929
  • D. 1930

53. Jinnah's Fourteen Points were announced in:

  • A. 1928
  • B. 1929

  • D. 1931
Answer: B — 1929. The Fourteen Points were presented in March 1929.

54. The Round Table Conferences were held in London during:

  • A. 1928–30
  • B. 1930–32
  • C. 1932–34
  • D. 1935–37
Answer: B — 1930–32. Three Round Table Conferences were held in 1930–1932.

55. The Government of India Act 1935 introduced:

  • A. Dyarchy in provinces ended; provincial autonomy
  • B. Complete independence
  • C. One Unit
  • D. Martial law
Answer: A — Dyarchy in provinces ended; provincial autonomy. The 1935 Act gave provincial autonomy and an All-India federation scheme.

56. The 1937 provincial elections gave Congress ministries in how many provinces?

58. Pakistan's first Constituent Assembly had how many members (initially)?

59. The first President of Pakistan was:

60. The capital city Islamabad was officially named in:

  • A. 1959

Islamiyat

61. The first migration of Muslims was to Abyssinia in which year of Nabawi?

  • A. 3rd
  • B. 5th
  • C. 7th
  • D. 10th
Answer: B — 5th. The first Hijrah to Abyssinia took place in 5 Nabawi.

62. The Prophet's (PBUH) year of birth is known as:

  • A. Aam-ul-Feel (Year of the Elephant)
  • B. Aam-ul-Huzn
  • C. Aam-ur-Ramada
  • D. Aam-ul-Fath
Answer: A — Aam-ul-Feel (Year of the Elephant). He was born in the Year of the Elephant (570 CE).

63. The number of Sahaba (companions) who participated in Badr was:

  • A. 300
  • B. 313
  • C. 317
  • D. 305
Answer: B — 313. 313 companions took part in Badr.

64. Hazrat Fatimah (RA) was the ___ daughter of the Prophet (PBUH):

  • A. Eldest
  • B. Youngest
  • C. Second
  • D. Third
Answer: B — Youngest. Hazrat Fatimah (RA) was his youngest daughter.

65. The treaty with the Jews of Madinah is part of:

  • A. Misaq-e-Madinah
  • B. Sulh Hudaybiyyah
  • C. Bait-ul-Aqaba
  • D. Fath-e-Makkah
Answer: A — Misaq-e-Madinah. The Charter of Madinah regulated relations with all Madinah tribes.

66. Surah An-Nas is the ___ Surah of the Quran:

  • A. First

  • C. 112th

67. The total number of Makki and Madani Surahs is:

  • A. 114
  • B. 113

68. The word 'Islam' literally means:

  • A. Peace and submission
  • B. Religion

69. The first Kalimah is called:

  • C. Israfil
  • D. Izrail
Answer: B — Mikail. Hazrat Mikail (AS) is in charge of rain and rizq (sustenance).

Basic Mathematics

71. What is 45 + 78 − 23?

  • A. 100
  • B. 90
  • C. 110
  • D. 96
Answer: A — 100. 45 + 78 = 123; 123 − 23 = 100.

72. The product of 12 × 15 is:

  • A. 160
  • B. 170
  • C. 180
  • D. 190
Answer: C — 180. 12 × 15 = 180.

73. What is 3/5 of 100?

  • A. 50
  • B. 55
  • C. 60
  • D. 65
Answer: C — 60. 3/5 × 100 = 60.

74. If a clerk types 40 words per minute, in 15 minutes he types:

  • A. 500 words
  • B. 600 words
  • C. 550 words
  • D. 650 words
Answer: B — 600 words. 40 × 15 = 600 words.

75. The average of 20, 30 and 40 is:

  • A. 25
  • B. 30

76. A file has 250 pages; 60% are printed. Printed pages are:

  • A. 120
