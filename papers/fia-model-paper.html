English — Vocabulary & Grammar

1. Choose the synonym of 'Investigate':

  • A. Ignore
  • B. Examine
  • C. Hide
  • D. Punish
Answer: B — Examine. To investigate means to examine carefully.

2. Choose the antonym of 'Guilty':

  • A. Criminal
  • B. Innocent
  • C. Accused
  • D. Sorry
Answer: B — Innocent. Guilty's opposite is innocent.

3. The correct spelling is:

  • A. Occurence
  • B. Occurrence
  • C. Ocurrence
  • D. Occurrance
Answer: B — Occurrence. Occurrence has double 'c' and double 'r'.

4. The suspect was arrested ___ the police.

  • A. from
  • B. by
  • C. with
  • D. of
Answer: B — by. In passive constructions, the agent is introduced by 'by'.

5. Choose the correct sentence:

  • A. The police has arrested him.
  • B. The police have arrested him.
  • C. The police is arrested him.
  • D. The police was arrest him.
Answer: B — The police have arrested him.. 'Police' is treated as plural: 'have arrested'.

6. One word: 'The study of crime and criminals' is:

  • A. Criminology
  • B. Penology
  • C. Victimology
  • D. Sociology
Answer: A — Criminology. Criminology is the study of crime and criminals.

7. The idiom 'to take the law into one's own hands' means:

  • A. To become a judge
  • B. To punish others illegally oneself
  • C. To study law
  • D. To call the police
Answer: B — To punish others illegally oneself. It means to act as one's own judge/punisher outside the law.

8. He was accused ___ theft.

  • A. for
  • B. with
  • C. of
  • D. about
Answer: C — of. We say 'accused of' a crime.

9. Choose the synonym of 'Evidence':

  • A. Proof
  • B. Doubt
  • C. Rumor
  • D. Claim
Answer: A — Proof. Evidence means proof or facts showing truth.

10. The witness gave a detailed ___ of the incident.

  • A. account
  • B. amount
  • C. accord
  • D. account for
Answer: A — account. An 'account' is a description of events.

11. If I ___ the truth, I would tell you.

  • A. know
  • B. knew
  • C. known
  • D. knows
Answer: B — knew. Second conditional uses past simple: 'If I knew...'.

12. Choose the antonym of 'Legal':

  • A. Lawful
  • B. Illegal
  • C. Valid
  • D. Official
Answer: B — Illegal. Legal's opposite is illegal.

13. The report was written ___ the inspector.

  • A. with
  • B. from
  • C. by
  • D. through of
Answer: C — by. Passive voice uses 'by' for the doer.

14. One word: 'A person who sees an event happen' is a:

  • A. Suspect
  • B. Witness
  • C. Victim
  • D. Clerk
Answer: B — Witness. A witness is someone who saw the event.

15. She denied ___ the money.

  • A. to take
  • B. taking
  • C. take
  • D. taken
Answer: B — taking. 'Deny' is followed by the -ing form: denied taking.

16. The plural of 'Crisis' is:

  • A. Crisises
  • B. Crises
  • C. Crisen
  • D. Crisis
Answer: B — Crises. Crisis → crises (Greek plural).

17. Choose the correct sentence (one of the + plural):

  • A. He is one of the best officer.
  • B. He is one of the best officers.
  • C. He is best officers.
  • D. He is one of best officer.
Answer: B — He is one of the best officers.. 'One of the' is followed by a plural noun.

18. The idiom 'in cold blood' means:

  • A. Slowly
  • B. Cruelly and without emotion
  • C. In winter
  • D. Secretly
Answer: B — Cruelly and without emotion. 'In cold blood' means ruthlessly, without feeling.

19. A brief summary of a document is called:

  • A. Draft
  • B. Abstract
  • C. Appendix
  • D. Index
Answer: B — Abstract. An abstract is a short summary of a document.

20. They have been investigating the case ___ March.

  • A. for
  • B. since
  • C. from
  • D. by
Answer: B — since. 'Since' points to the starting point in time.

General Knowledge

21. The headquarters of Interpol is located in:

  • A. Paris
  • B. Lyon
  • C. Geneva
  • D. Vienna
Answer: B — Lyon. Interpol's General Secretariat is in Lyon, France.

22. The first country to grant women the right to vote was:

  • A. USA
  • B. UK
  • C. New Zealand
  • D. France
Answer: C — New Zealand. New Zealand granted women's suffrage in 1893.

23. The longest wall in the world is in:

  • A. India
  • B. China
  • C. Pakistan
  • D. Iran
Answer: B — China. The Great Wall of China is the longest wall.

24. Which is the deepest ocean trench?

  • A. Java Trench
  • B. Mariana Trench
  • C. Puerto Rico Trench
  • D. Tonga Trench
Answer: B — Mariana Trench. The Mariana Trench is the deepest, about 11 km.

25. The book 'Mein Kampf' was written by:

  • A. Mussolini
  • B. Hitler
  • C. Stalin
  • D. Churchill
Answer: B — Hitler. Adolf Hitler wrote Mein Kampf.

26. The first human to travel in space was:

  • A. Neil Armstrong
  • B. Yuri Gagarin
  • C. Valentina Tereshkova
  • D. Alan Shepard
Answer: B — Yuri Gagarin. Yuri Gagarin orbited Earth in 1961.

27. The currency of Turkey is:

  • A. Lira
  • B. Dinar
  • C. Pound
  • D. Krona
Answer: A — Lira. Turkey's currency is the lira.

28. The International Court of Justice sits at:

  • A. New York
  • B. Geneva
  • C. The Hague
  • D. Brussels
Answer: C — The Hague. The ICJ is at the Peace Palace, The Hague, Netherlands.

29. How many provinces are there in Pakistan (including Gilgit-Baltistan as a region)?

  • A. 4 provinces
  • B. 5 provinces
  • C. 6 provinces
  • D. 7 provinces
Answer: A — 4 provinces. Pakistan has four provinces; GB and AJK are separate administered territories.

30. The famous painting 'Mona Lisa' was painted by:

  • A. Picasso
  • B. Van Gogh
  • C. Leonardo da Vinci
  • D. Michelangelo
Answer: C — Leonardo da Vinci. Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa.

31. The Red Cross was founded by:

  • A. Henry Dunant
  • B. Florence Nightingale
  • C. Clara Barton
  • D. Louis Pasteur
Answer: A — Henry Dunant. Henry Dunant founded the Red Cross in 1863.

32. Which country is both a continent and a country?

  • A. Canada
  • B. Australia
  • C. Brazil
  • D. Russia
Answer: B — Australia. Australia is the only country that is also a continent.

33. The 'Forbidden City' is located in:

  • A. Tokyo
  • B. Beijing
  • C. Seoul
  • D. Bangkok
Answer: B — Beijing. The Forbidden City is in Beijing, China.

34. The vitamin that helps in the absorption of calcium is:

  • A. Vitamin A
  • B. Vitamin B
  • C. Vitamin C
  • D. Vitamin D
Answer: D — Vitamin D. Vitamin D aids calcium absorption for bones.

35. The largest exporter of oil in the world (historically leading) is:

  • A. Russia
  • B. Saudi Arabia
  • C. USA
  • D. Iraq
Answer: B — Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia has long been among the largest crude oil exporters.

Pakistan Studies

36. The FIA (Federal Investigation Agency) was established in:

  • A. 1971
  • B. 1975
  • C. 1979
  • D. 1985
Answer: B — 1975. FIA was created under the FIA Act 1974 and established in January 1975.

37. The FIA Act was passed in the year:

  • A. 1973
  • B. 1974
  • C. 1975
  • D. 1976
Answer: B — 1974. The Federal Investigation Agency Act was enacted in 1974; the Agency began work in January 1975.

38. The national language of Pakistan is:

  • A. Punjabi
  • B. Urdu
  • C. Sindhi
  • D. English
Answer: B — Urdu. Urdu is the national language; English is the official language.

39. Pakistan's first television station (PTV) started in:

  • A. 1960
  • B. 1964
  • C. 1968
  • D. 1970
Answer: B — 1964. PTV began transmissions from Lahore in November 1964.

40. The Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila) was largely built during the reign of:

  • A. Babur
  • B. Akbar
  • C. Sher Shah
  • D. Humayun
Answer: B — Akbar. Akbar rebuilt the Lahore Fort in its Mughal form.

41. The 'Pakistan Day' is celebrated on:

  • A. 14 August
  • B. 23 March
  • C. 6 September
  • D. 25 December
Answer: B — 23 March. Pakistan Day (Yaum-e-Pakistan) is 23 March.

42. Defence Day of Pakistan is observed on:

  • A. 6 September
  • B. 7 September
  • C. 14 August
  • D. 23 March
Answer: A — 6 September. Defence Day is observed on 6 September (1965 war).

43. The first woman governor of the State Bank of Pakistan was:

  • A. Shamshad Akhtar
  • B. Hina Rabbani Khar
  • C. Fehmida Mirza
  • D. Kashmala Tariq
Answer: A — Shamshad Akhtar. Dr Shamshad Akhtar became the first woman SBP Governor in 2006.

44. Pakistan's first postage stamp was issued in:

  • A. 1947
  • B. 1948
  • C. 1949
  • D. 1950
Answer: B — 1948. Pakistan's first stamps were issued in July 1948.

45. The name 'Pakistan' was coined by:

  • A. Allama Iqbal
  • B. Chaudhry Rehmat Ali
  • C. Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
  • D. Quaid-e-Azam
Answer: B — Chaudhry Rehmat Ali. Chaudhry Rehmat Ali coined 'Pakistan' in his 1933 pamphlet 'Now or Never'.

Islamiyat

46. The first Surah revealed completely was:

  • A. Al-Fatihah
  • B. Al-Alaq
  • C. Al-Muddaththir
  • D. Al-Qalam
Answer: A — Al-Fatihah. Surah Al-Fatihah is regarded as the first complete Surah revealed (Al-Alaq's first verses came first).

47. The number of verses in Surah Al-Fatihah is:

  • A. 5
  • B. 6
  • C. 7
  • D. 8
Answer: C — 7. Surah Al-Fatihah has 7 verses.

48. Hazrat Hamza (RA) was martyred in the Battle of:

  • A. Badr
  • B. Uhud
  • C. Khandaq
  • D. Hunayn
Answer: B — Uhud. Hazrat Hamza (RA) was martyred at Uhud (3 AH).

49. The Prophet (PBUH) performed the Farewell Hajj in:

  • A. 8 AH
  • B. 9 AH
  • C. 10 AH
  • D. 11 AH
Answer: C — 10 AH. The Farewell Hajj was in 10 AH (632 CE).

50. The book 'Sahih Muslim' was compiled by:

  • A. Imam Bukhari
  • B. Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj
  • C. Imam Malik
  • D. Imam Shafi'i
Answer: B — Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj. Imam Muslim compiled Sahih Muslim.

51. The first Masjid built in Islam is:

  • A. Masjid-e-Nabvi
  • B. Masjid-e-Quba
  • C. Masjid-e-Haram
  • D. Masjid-e-Aqsa
Answer: B — Masjid-e-Quba. Masjid Quba in Madinah was the first mosque built.

52. Zakat on cash/gold is charged at the rate of:

  • A. 2%
  • B. 2.5%
  • C. 5%
  • D. 10%
Answer: B — 2.5%. Zakat on money and gold/silver is 2.5% (one-fortieth).

53. The event of 'Isra and Miraj' took place in which month (widely observed)?

  • A. Ramadan
  • B. Rajab
  • C. Shaban
  • D. Rabi-ul-Awwal
Answer: B — Rajab. Miraj is widely observed on 27 Rajab.

54. How many daughters did the Prophet (PBUH) have?

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5
Answer: C — 4. He had four daughters: Zainab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum and Fatimah (RA).

55. The first revealed word of the Quran was:

  • A. Qalam
  • B. Iqra
  • C. Alhamd
  • D. Rahman
Answer: B — Iqra. The first revealed word was 'Iqra' (Read).

Basic Mathematics

56. What is 12.5% of 800?

  • A. 80
  • B. 90
  • C. 100
  • D. 120
Answer: C — 100. 12.5% = 1/8; 800 ÷ 8 = 100.

57. If 3x = 27, then x² = ?

  • A. 9
  • B. 27
  • C. 81
  • D. 729
Answer: C — 81. x = 9, so x² = 81.

58. The average of 8, 12, 16 and 24 is:

  • A. 14
  • B. 15
  • C. 16
  • D. 18
Answer: B — 15. (8+12+16+24) ÷ 4 = 60 ÷ 4 = 15.

59. A man walks 5 km in 1 hour and 3 km in the next hour. His average speed is:

  • A. 3.5 km/h
  • B. 4 km/h
  • C. 4.5 km/h
  • D. 5 km/h
Answer: B — 4 km/h. Total 8 km in 2 hours = 4 km/h.

60. The next term in 1, 4, 9, 16, 25 is:

  • A. 30
  • B. 36
  • C. 49
  • D. 32
Answer: B — 36. Squares: 1²..5²; next is 6² = 36.

61. Rs 6,000 at 10% simple interest for 3 years gives interest of:

  • A. Rs 1,200
  • B. Rs 1,500
  • C. Rs 1,800
  • D. Rs 2,000
Answer: C — Rs 1,800. SI = 6000 × 10 × 3 ÷ 100 = Rs 1,800.

62. If a:b = 2:5 and a = 10, then b = ?

  • A. 20
  • B. 25
  • C. 15
  • D. 30
Answer: B — 25. Scale factor 5; b = 5 × 5 = 25.

63. The value of √625 is:

  • A. 15
  • B. 20
  • C. 25
  • D. 35
Answer: C — 25. 25 × 25 = 625.

64. A triangle has sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. Its perimeter is:

  • A. 10 cm
  • B. 11 cm
  • C. 12 cm
  • D. 13 cm
Answer: C — 12 cm. Perimeter = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 cm.

65. What is 0.75 × 0.4?

  • A. 0.03
  • B. 0.30
  • C. 3.0
  • D. 0.115
Answer: B — 0.30. 0.75 × 0.4 = 0.30.

66. The HCF of 18 and 27 is:

  • A. 3
  • B. 6
  • C. 9
  • D. 18
Answer: C — 9. Common factors of 18 and 27 peak at 9.

67. A price rises from Rs 400 to Rs 500. The percentage increase is:

  • A. 20%
  • B. 25%
  • C. 15%
  • D. 30%
Answer: B — 25%. Increase = 100; 100 ÷ 400 × 100 = 25%.

68. If 20 men can do a work in 15 days, 30 men will do it in:

  • A. 10 days
  • B. 12 days
  • C. 22 days
  • D. 25 days
Answer: A — 10 days. Work = 300 man-days; 300 ÷ 30 = 10 days.

69. The sum of the first 10 even numbers is:

  • A. 100
  • B. 110
  • C. 90
  • D. 120
Answer: B — 110. 2+4+...+20 = 2(1+...+10) = 2 × 55 = 110.

70. The area of a circle with radius 7 cm (π = 22/7) is:

  • A. 154 cm²
  • B. 144 cm²
  • C. 164 cm²
  • D. 134 cm²
Answer: A — 154 cm². Area = 22/7 × 49 = 154 cm².

Everyday Science

71. Which blood group is called the universal donor?

  • A. AB positive
  • B. O negative
  • C. O positive
  • D. AB negative
Answer: B — O negative. O negative blood can be given to patients of any group in emergencies.

72. The chemical symbol of lead is:

  • A. Ld
  • B. Pb
  • C. Le
  • D. Pl
Answer: B — Pb. Pb comes from 'plumbum', Latin for lead.

73. Which planet has the largest ring system?

  • A. Jupiter
  • B. Uranus
  • C. Saturn
  • D. Neptune
Answer: C — Saturn. Saturn's rings are the largest and brightest.

74. The energy stored in food is measured in:

  • A. Joules only
  • B. Calories
  • C. Volts
  • D. Watts
Answer: B — Calories. Food energy is measured in calories (kilocalories).

75. Which acid is found in lemon?

  • A. Acetic acid
  • B. Citric acid
  • C. Sulphuric acid
  • D. Nitric acid
Answer: B — Citric acid. Lemons contain citric acid.

76. The instrument used to measure temperature is:

  • A. Barometer
  • B. Thermometer
  • C. Speedometer
  • D. Altimeter
Answer: B — Thermometer. A thermometer measures temperature.

77. Which layer of the atmosphere protects us from ultraviolet rays?

  • A. Troposphere
  • B. Ozone layer
  • C. Ionosphere
  • D. Exosphere
Answer: B — Ozone layer. The ozone layer absorbs most harmful UV radiation.

78. The hardest part of the human body is:

  • A. Bone
  • B. Tooth enamel
  • C. Hair
  • D. Nail
Answer: B — Tooth enamel. Tooth enamel is the hardest substance in the body.

79. Which of these diseases is caused by a virus?

  • A. Typhoid
  • B. Cholera
  • C. Influenza
  • D. Malaria
Answer: C — Influenza. Influenza (flu) is caused by a virus; typhoid and cholera are bacterial, malaria is parasitic.

80. The process of conversion of water into vapour is called:

  • A. Condensation
  • B. Evaporation
  • C. Freezing
  • D. Sublimation
Answer: B — Evaporation. Evaporation turns liquid water into vapour.

Basic Computer

81. The full form of HTTP is:

  • A. Hyper Text Transfer Protocol
  • B. High Text Transfer Protocol
  • C. Hyper Text Transmission Program
  • D. Host Text Transfer Protocol
Answer: A — Hyper Text Transfer Protocol. HTTP = HyperText Transfer Protocol.

82. Which part of the computer performs arithmetic and logic operations?

  • A. CU
  • B. ALU
  • C. MU
  • D. Register
Answer: B — ALU. The ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) does calculations and logic.

83. One kilobyte (KB) is equal to:

  • A. 1000 bytes
  • B. 1024 bytes
  • C. 512 bytes
  • D. 2048 bytes
Answer: B — 1024 bytes. 1 KB = 1024 bytes in the binary system used by computers.

84. Which of these is NOT an input device?

  • A. Joystick
  • B. Microphone
  • C. Printer
  • D. Webcam
Answer: C — Printer. A printer is an output device.

85. In MS PowerPoint, slides are used for:

  • A. Typing letters
  • B. Presentations
  • C. Calculations
  • D. Databases
Answer: B — Presentations. PowerPoint is presentation software.

86. The brain of any computer system is:

  • A. ALU
  • B. Memory
  • C. CPU
  • D. Control Unit
Answer: C — CPU. The CPU as a whole is called the brain of the computer.

87. Which shortcut is used to select all text?

  • A. Ctrl + S
  • B. Ctrl + A
  • C. Ctrl + E
  • D. Ctrl + R
Answer: B — Ctrl + A. Ctrl + A selects all content.

88. A 'firewall' in computing is used for:

  • A. Speeding the internet
  • B. Security against unauthorized access
  • C. Printing
  • D. Backup
Answer: B — Security against unauthorized access. A firewall protects a network from unauthorized access.

89. The file extension '.xlsx' belongs to:

  • A. Word
  • B. Excel
  • C. PowerPoint
  • D. Access
Answer: B — Excel. Excel workbooks use the .xlsx extension.

90. Which company developed the Windows operating system?

  • A. Apple
  • B. Google
  • C. Microsoft
  • D. IBM
Answer: C — Microsoft. Microsoft developed Windows.

Current Affairs & FIA Basics

91. The FIA mainly investigates crimes related to:

  • A. Only traffic offences
  • B. Cybercrime, human smuggling, money laundering and corruption (federal offences)
  • C. Village disputes
  • D. School exams
Answer: B — Cybercrime, human smuggling, money laundering and corruption (federal offences). FIA handles federal offences: cybercrime, human smuggling/trafficking, money laundering, immigration and corruption.

92. FIA's headquarters is located in:

  • A. Karachi
  • B. Lahore
  • C. Islamabad
  • D. Peshawar
Answer: C — Islamabad. FIA Headquarters is in Islamabad.

93. The Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) was passed in:

  • A. 2014
  • B. 2016
  • C. 2018
  • D. 2020
Answer: B — 2016. PECA was enacted in 2016 to deal with cybercrime in Pakistan.

94. Cybercrime in Pakistan is investigated primarily by:

  • A. Police stations
  • B. FIA Cybercrime Wing
  • C. Customs
  • D. ASF
Answer: B — FIA Cybercrime Wing. The FIA Cybercrime Wing investigates offences under PECA.

95. Pakistan's first cybercrime law was introduced through:

  • A. PPC 1860
  • B. PECA 2016
  • C. FIA Act 1974 only
  • D. Companies Act
Answer: B — PECA 2016. PECA 2016 is Pakistan's dedicated cybercrime law.

96. Human smuggling/trafficking cases at airports are handled by FIA's:

  • A. Economic Crime Wing
  • B. Immigration Wing / Anti Human Smuggling circles
  • C. Technical Wing
  • D. Legal Wing
Answer: B — Immigration Wing / Anti Human Smuggling circles. FIA's Immigration and Anti-Human-Smuggling circles work at airports and borders.

97. The Anti-Money Laundering Act of Pakistan was enacted in:

  • A. 2007
  • B. 2010
  • C. 2015
  • D. 2020
Answer: B — 2010. The Anti-Money Laundering Act was passed in 2010.

98. Pakistan is a founding-region member of the FATF-style regional body called:

  • A. APG
  • B. EAG only
  • C. MENAFATF
  • D. ESAAMLG
Answer: A — APG. Pakistan is a member of the Asia/Pacific Group on Money Laundering (APG).

99. The official emergency helpline for FIA cybercrime complaints (online portal) is through:

  • A. NADRA website
  • B. FIA Cybercrime reporting portal / 1991 helpline
  • C. PTA only
  • D. Police 15 only
Answer: B — FIA Cybercrime reporting portal / 1991 helpline. FIA cybercrime complaints can be lodged via its online portal and the 1991 helpline.

100. Pakistan's passport and immigration checks at airports are carried out by:

  • A. Airlines staff
  • B. FIA Immigration
  • C. Customs only
  • D. CAA only
Answer: B — FIA Immigration. FIA Immigration staff perform passport control at international airports.
