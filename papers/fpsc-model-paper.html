English — Vocabulary & Grammar

1. Choose the synonym of 'Abandon':

  • A. Keep
  • B. Leave
  • C. Hold
  • D. Build
Answer: B — Leave. Abandon means to leave or give up completely.

2. Choose the antonym of 'Ancient':

  • A. Old
  • B. Modern
  • C. Antique
  • D. Historic
Answer: B — Modern. Ancient means very old; its opposite is modern.

3. Choose the synonym of 'Rapid':

  • A. Slow
  • B. Steady
  • C. Swift
  • D. Weak
Answer: C — Swift. Rapid means fast or swift.

4. Choose the antonym of 'Expand':

  • A. Extend
  • B. Enlarge
  • C. Contract
  • D. Stretch
Answer: C — Contract. Expand means to grow bigger; contract means to shrink.

5. The correct spelling is:

  • A. Occassion
  • B. Occasion
  • C. Ocassion
  • D. Occation
Answer: B — Occasion. The correct spelling is 'occasion' with one 's' pair doubled c.

6. He has been living in Karachi ___ 2010.

  • A. for
  • B. since
  • C. from
  • D. by
Answer: B — since. 'Since' is used with a point of time, 'for' with a duration.

7. She is good ___ English.

  • A. in
  • B. at
  • C. on
  • D. for
Answer: B — at. We say 'good at' a subject or skill.

8. Choose the correct sentence:

  • A. He don't know the answer.
  • B. He doesn't knows the answer.
  • C. He doesn't know the answer.
  • D. He not know the answer.
Answer: C — He doesn't know the answer.. With 'doesn't', the main verb stays in its base form.

9. The passive voice of 'She writes a letter' is:

  • A. A letter is written by her.
  • B. A letter was written by her.
  • C. A letter is being write by her.
  • D. A letter has written by her.
Answer: A — A letter is written by her.. Present simple passive: is + past participle (written).

10. One word: 'A person who speaks many languages' is a:

  • A. Linguist
  • B. Polyglot
  • C. Orator
  • D. Bilingual
Answer: B — Polyglot. A polyglot knows or uses several languages.

11. Choose the synonym of 'Transparent':

  • A. Opaque
  • B. Clear
  • C. Thick
  • D. Dark
Answer: B — Clear. Transparent means clear enough to see through.

12. Choose the antonym of 'Victory':

  • A. Triumph
  • B. Success
  • C. Defeat
  • D. Glory
Answer: C — Defeat. Victory's opposite is defeat.

13. Neither the teacher nor the students ___ present.

  • A. was
  • B. were
  • C. is
  • D. has
Answer: B — were. With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject (students — plural).

14. He died ___ malaria.

  • A. from
  • B. of
  • C. with
  • D. by
Answer: B — of. We say 'die of' a disease.

15. The idiom 'to bite the dust' means:

  • A. To eat quickly
  • B. To fail or fall
  • C. To work hard
  • D. To hide something
Answer: B — To fail or fall. 'Bite the dust' means to fail, fall or die.

16. Choose the correct spelling:

  • A. Seperate
  • B. Separate
  • C. Seprate
  • D. Separrate
Answer: B — Separate. The correct spelling is 'separate' — remember 'a rat' in the middle.

17. A group of wolves is called a:

  • A. Herd
  • B. Pack
  • C. Flock
  • D. Swarm
Answer: B — Pack. Wolves live and hunt in a pack.

18. The feminine of 'Hero' is:

  • A. Heroine
  • B. Herowoman
  • C. Heroess
  • D. Lady hero
Answer: A — Heroine. The feminine form of hero is heroine.

19. I wish I ___ a bird.

  • A. am
  • B. was
  • C. were
  • D. be
Answer: C — were. In wishes contrary to fact, 'were' is used for all persons.

20. He asked me where I ___ .

  • A. live
  • B. lives
  • C. lived
  • D. am living
Answer: C — lived. Reported speech shifts the verb one step back (live → lived).

General Knowledge

21. The largest continent of the world by area is:

  • A. Africa
  • B. Asia
  • C. Europe
  • D. North America
Answer: B — Asia. Asia is the largest continent, covering about 30% of Earth's land.

22. The currency of Japan is:

  • A. Won
  • B. Yuan
  • C. Yen
  • D. Ringgit
Answer: C — Yen. Japan's currency is the yen.

23. The headquarters of the United Nations is in:

  • A. Geneva
  • B. New York
  • C. Paris
  • D. Vienna
Answer: B — New York. The UN headquarters is in New York City, USA.

24. Which is the longest river in the world?

  • A. Amazon
  • B. Yangtze
  • C. Nile
  • D. Mississippi
Answer: C — Nile. The Nile (about 6,650 km) is generally regarded as the longest river.

25. The first man to step on the Moon was:

  • A. Yuri Gagarin
  • B. Neil Armstrong
  • C. Buzz Aldrin
  • D. Michael Collins
Answer: B — Neil Armstrong. Neil Armstrong landed on the Moon on 20 July 1969.

26. The chemical symbol of gold is:

  • A. Ag
  • B. Fe
  • C. Au
  • D. Gd
Answer: C — Au. Au comes from 'aurum', the Latin word for gold.

27. The Great Wall is located in:

  • A. Japan
  • B. China
  • C. Korea
  • D. Mongolia
Answer: B — China. The Great Wall of China stretches across northern China.

28. Which country is called the 'Land of the Rising Sun'?

  • A. China
  • B. Thailand
  • C. Japan
  • D. Philippines
Answer: C — Japan. Japan lies east of Asia, where the sun 'rises' first.

29. The largest ocean of the world is:

  • A. Atlantic
  • B. Indian
  • C. Arctic
  • D. Pacific
Answer: D — Pacific. The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean.

30. The first President of the United States was:

  • A. Abraham Lincoln
  • B. George Washington
  • C. Thomas Jefferson
  • D. John Adams
Answer: B — George Washington. George Washington served as the first US President (1789–1797).

31. The national game of Pakistan is:

  • A. Cricket
  • B. Hockey
  • C. Football
  • D. Squash
Answer: B — Hockey. Field hockey is Pakistan's national game.

32. Which vitamin is produced in the skin by sunlight?

  • A. Vitamin A
  • B. Vitamin B12
  • C. Vitamin C
  • D. Vitamin D
Answer: D — Vitamin D. Sunlight helps the skin produce Vitamin D.

33. The world's largest desert (hot) is:

  • A. Gobi
  • B. Sahara
  • C. Arabian
  • D. Kalahari
Answer: B — Sahara. The Sahara in Africa is the largest hot desert.

34. The Olympic Games are held every:

  • A. 2 years
  • B. 3 years
  • C. 4 years
  • D. 5 years
Answer: C — 4 years. The modern Olympics are held every four years.

35. The author of 'Shahnama' is:

  • A. Firdowsi
  • B. Saadi
  • C. Rumi
  • D. Hafiz
Answer: A — Firdowsi. Ferdowsi (Firdowsi) wrote the Persian epic Shahnama.

36. Which gas makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon dioxide
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Argon
Answer: C — Nitrogen. Nitrogen is about 78% of the air we breathe.

37. The smallest country in the world is:

  • A. Monaco
  • B. Maldives
  • C. Vatican City
  • D. San Marino
Answer: C — Vatican City. Vatican City (about 0.44 km²) is the smallest state.

38. The Battle of Waterloo (1815) was fought in present-day:

  • A. France
  • B. Belgium
  • C. Germany
  • D. Netherlands
Answer: B — Belgium. Waterloo is in Belgium; Napoleon was defeated there in 1815.

39. The currency of the United Kingdom is:

  • A. Euro
  • B. Dollar
  • C. Pound Sterling
  • D. Franc
Answer: C — Pound Sterling. The UK uses the pound sterling.

40. The organ that pumps blood through the human body is:

  • A. Liver
  • B. Brain
  • C. Heart
  • D. Lung
Answer: C — Heart. The heart pumps blood around the body.

Pakistan Studies

41. The Pakistan Resolution was passed on:

  • A. 23 March 1940
  • B. 14 August 1947
  • C. 3 June 1947
  • D. 11 September 1948
Answer: A — 23 March 1940. The Lahore (Pakistan) Resolution was adopted on 23 March 1940.

42. The first Constitution of Pakistan was enforced in:

  • A. 1947
  • B. 1956
  • C. 1962
  • D. 1973
Answer: B — 1956. Pakistan's first constitution came into force on 23 March 1956.

43. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah was born in:

  • A. Lahore
  • B. Delhi
  • C. Karachi
  • D. Bombay
Answer: C — Karachi. Jinnah was born in Karachi on 25 December 1876.

44. The Simla Deputation met Lord Minto in:

  • A. 1905
  • B. 1906
  • C. 1909
  • D. 1911
Answer: B — 1906. The Simla Deputation of Muslim leaders met the Viceroy in October 1906.

45. The All-India Muslim League was founded in:

  • A. Lahore
  • B. Aligarh
  • C. Dhaka
  • D. Delhi
Answer: C — Dhaka. The Muslim League was founded at Dhaka on 30 December 1906.

46. Pakistan's first Governor-General was:

  • A. Liaquat Ali Khan
  • B. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  • C. Khawaja Nazimuddin
  • D. Ghulam Muhammad
Answer: B — Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah. Jinnah became the first Governor-General of Pakistan in 1947.

47. The Objective Resolution was passed in:

  • A. 1947
  • B. 1949
  • C. 1951
  • D. 1956
Answer: B — 1949. The Objectives Resolution was adopted on 12 March 1949.

48. The Indus Waters Treaty between Pakistan and India was signed in:

  • A. 1958
  • B. 1960
  • C. 1965
  • D. 1972
Answer: B — 1960. The Indus Waters Treaty was signed in Karachi in September 1960.

49. The present Constitution of Pakistan (1973) was enforced on:

  • A. 23 March 1973
  • B. 14 August 1973
  • C. 25 December 1973
  • D. 1 January 1974
Answer: B — 14 August 1973. The 1973 Constitution came into force on 14 August 1973.

50. The national anthem of Pakistan was written by:

  • A. Allama Iqbal
  • B. Hafeez Jalandhari
  • C. Faiz Ahmed Faiz
  • D. Josh Malihabadi
Answer: B — Hafeez Jalandhari. Hafeez Jalandhari wrote the national anthem (Qaumi Tarana).

51. Pakistan's largest province by population is:

  • A. Sindh
  • B. Balochistan
  • C. Punjab
  • D. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Answer: C — Punjab. Punjab is the most populous province; Balochistan is largest by area.

52. The first Prime Minister of Pakistan was:

  • A. Quaid-e-Azam
  • B. Liaquat Ali Khan
  • C. Khawaja Nazimuddin
  • D. Muhammad Ali Bogra
Answer: B — Liaquat Ali Khan. Liaquat Ali Khan was Pakistan's first Prime Minister.

53. Pakistan became a republic on:

  • A. 14 August 1947
  • B. 23 March 1956
  • C. 16 December 1971
  • D. 14 August 1973
Answer: B — 23 March 1956. Pakistan became an Islamic Republic on 23 March 1956.

54. The Lucknow Pact was signed in:

  • A. 1906
  • B. 1913
  • C. 1916
  • D. 1919
Answer: C — 1916. The Lucknow Pact (Congress–League agreement) was signed in 1916.

55. Pakistan's national flower is:

  • A. Rose
  • B. Jasmine
  • C. Tulip
  • D. Sunflower
Answer: B — Jasmine. Jasmine (Chambeli) is Pakistan's national flower.

Islamiyat

56. The first Surah of the Holy Quran is:

  • A. Al-Baqarah
  • B. Al-Fatihah
  • C. Al-Ikhlas
  • D. An-Nas
Answer: B — Al-Fatihah. Surah Al-Fatihah is the opening chapter of the Quran.

57. How many Surahs are there in the Holy Quran?

  • A. 110
  • B. 114
  • C. 116
  • D. 120
Answer: B — 114. The Holy Quran has 114 Surahs.

58. The longest Surah of the Quran is:

  • A. Aal-e-Imran
  • B. An-Nisa
  • C. Al-Baqarah
  • D. Al-A'raf
Answer: C — Al-Baqarah. Surah Al-Baqarah, with 286 verses, is the longest.

59. The first revelation was revealed in the cave of:

  • A. Thawr
  • B. Hira
  • C. Nur
  • D. Safa
Answer: B — Hira. The first revelation came to the Prophet (PBUH) in Cave Hira.

60. The Battle of Badr was fought in:

  • A. 1 AH
  • B. 2 AH
  • C. 3 AH
  • D. 5 AH
Answer: B — 2 AH. Badr was fought on 17 Ramadan, 2 AH (624 CE).

61. How many Muslims fought in the Battle of Badr?

  • A. 313
  • B. 700
  • C. 1000
  • D. 3000
Answer: A — 313. 313 Muslims faced about 1,000 Quraysh at Badr.

62. The first Caliph of Islam was:

  • A. Hazrat Umar (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Usman (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Ali (RA)
Answer: B — Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA). Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) was the first Caliph.

63. Zakat is payable on silver at the Nisab of:

  • A. 52.5 tola
  • B. 7.5 tola
  • C. 87.5 gram
  • D. 40 tola
Answer: A — 52.5 tola. The Nisab for silver is 52.5 tola (612.36 grams).

64. The number of obligatory (Fard) Rak'ahs in Fajr prayer is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 1
Answer: A — 2. Fajr has 2 Fard Rak'ahs.

65. Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) was born in:

  • A. Madinah
  • B. Makkah
  • C. Taif
  • D. Jerusalem
Answer: B — Makkah. The Prophet (PBUH) was born in Makkah in 570 CE.

66. The Quran was first collected in book form during the caliphate of:

  • A. Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Umar (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Usman (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Ali (RA)
Answer: A — Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA). The Quran was compiled into one book under Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA).

67. The standard copy of the Quran was prepared under:

  • A. Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Umar (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Usman (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Ali (RA)
Answer: C — Hazrat Usman (RA). Hazrat Usman (RA) prepared the standard recension of the Quran.

68. The month in which the Quran was revealed is:

  • A. Rajab
  • B. Shaban
  • C. Ramadan
  • D. Muharram
Answer: C — Ramadan. The Quran was revealed in Ramadan (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:185).

69. The first Hijri year corresponds to the Prophet's migration to:

  • A. Taif
  • B. Madinah
  • C. Abyssinia
  • D. Jerusalem
Answer: B — Madinah. The Hijri calendar begins with the migration to Madinah (622 CE).

70. The number of Makki Surahs is generally counted as:

  • A. 82
  • B. 86
  • C. 90
  • D. 28
Answer: B — 86. 86 Surahs are Makki and 28 are Madani (a widely used count).

Basic Mathematics

71. What is 15% of 300?

  • A. 30
  • B. 40
  • C. 45
  • D. 60
Answer: C — 45. 15% of 300 = 0.15 × 300 = 45.

72. The average of 12, 18 and 30 is:

  • A. 18
  • B. 20
  • C. 22
  • D. 25
Answer: B — 20. (12+18+30) ÷ 3 = 60 ÷ 3 = 20.

73. If a train travels 180 km in 3 hours, its average speed is:

  • A. 50 km/h
  • B. 55 km/h
  • C. 60 km/h
  • D. 90 km/h
Answer: C — 60 km/h. Speed = 180 ÷ 3 = 60 km/h.

74. A man buys an item for Rs 800 and sells it for Rs 1,000. His profit percentage is:

  • A. 20%
  • B. 25%
  • C. 15%
  • D. 30%
Answer: B — 25%. Profit = 200; 200 ÷ 800 × 100 = 25%.

75. What is the next number in the series 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ___?

  • A. 36
  • B. 40
  • C. 42
  • D. 44
Answer: C — 42. Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10 — next difference 12 gives 42 (also n²+n).

76. If 5 workers complete a job in 12 days, in how many days will 10 workers complete it (same rate)?

  • A. 24 days
  • B. 10 days
  • C. 6 days
  • D. 8 days
Answer: C — 6 days. Work = 5 × 12 = 60 worker-days; 60 ÷ 10 = 6 days.

77. The simple interest on Rs 10,000 at 5% per year for 2 years is:

  • A. Rs 500
  • B. Rs 1,000
  • C. Rs 1,500
  • D. Rs 2,000
Answer: B — Rs 1,000. SI = 10,000 × 5 × 2 ÷ 100 = Rs 1,000.

78. What is 3/4 expressed as a percentage?

  • A. 65%
  • B. 70%
  • C. 75%
  • D. 80%
Answer: C — 75%. 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.

79. The HCF of 24 and 36 is:

  • A. 6
  • B. 8
  • C. 12
  • D. 18
Answer: C — 12. Common factors of 24 and 36 peak at 12.

80. A rectangle has length 12 cm and width 5 cm. Its area is:

  • A. 34 cm²
  • B. 60 cm²
  • C. 50 cm²
  • D. 17 cm²
Answer: B — 60 cm². Area = 12 × 5 = 60 cm².

Everyday Science

81. The powerhouse of the cell is:

  • A. Nucleus
  • B. Ribosome
  • C. Mitochondria
  • D. Golgi body
Answer: C — Mitochondria. Mitochondria produce the cell's energy (ATP).

82. Water is composed of hydrogen and oxygen in the ratio (by volume):

  • A. 1:2
  • B. 2:1
  • C. 1:1
  • D. 3:1
Answer: B — 2:1. Water (H₂O) has hydrogen and oxygen in a 2:1 ratio.

83. The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:

  • A. 300,000 km/s
  • B. 150,000 km/s
  • C. 30,000 km/s
  • D. 1,000 km/s
Answer: A — 300,000 km/s. Light travels at about 300,000 km/s in vacuum.

84. Which blood cells carry oxygen in the body?

  • A. White blood cells
  • B. Platelets
  • C. Red blood cells
  • D. Plasma cells
Answer: C — Red blood cells. Red blood cells carry oxygen via haemoglobin.

85. The process by which plants prepare food is called:

  • A. Respiration
  • B. Photosynthesis
  • C. Digestion
  • D. Transpiration
Answer: B — Photosynthesis. Photosynthesis converts sunlight, water and CO₂ into food.

86. The chemical formula of common salt is:

  • A. NaCl
  • B. KCl
  • C. CaCl₂
  • D. NaOH
Answer: A — NaCl. Common salt is sodium chloride, NaCl.

87. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

  • A. Venus
  • B. Mars
  • C. Jupiter
  • D. Mercury
Answer: B — Mars. Mars appears red due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

88. The normal human body temperature is about:

  • A. 35°C
  • B. 36°C
  • C. 37°C
  • D. 39°C
Answer: C — 37°C. Normal body temperature is about 37°C (98.6°F).

89. Sound cannot travel through:

  • A. Air
  • B. Water
  • C. Vacuum
  • D. Steel
Answer: C — Vacuum. Sound needs a medium; it cannot travel through a vacuum.

90. Which gas do plants absorb from the air for photosynthesis?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Nitrogen
  • C. Carbon dioxide
  • D. Hydrogen
Answer: C — Carbon dioxide. Plants absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen.

Basic Computer

91. The full form of CPU is:

  • A. Central Processing Unit
  • B. Computer Personal Unit
  • C. Central Program Unit
  • D. Central Peripheral Unit
Answer: A — Central Processing Unit. CPU stands for Central Processing Unit.

92. Which of the following is an input device?

  • A. Monitor
  • B. Printer
  • C. Keyboard
  • D. Speaker
Answer: C — Keyboard. A keyboard is used to input data into a computer.

93. One byte is equal to:

  • A. 4 bits
  • B. 8 bits
  • C. 16 bits
  • D. 32 bits
Answer: B — 8 bits. One byte = 8 bits.

94. The 'brain' of the computer is the:

  • A. Hard disk
  • B. CPU
  • C. Monitor
  • D. RAM
Answer: B — CPU. The CPU processes instructions and is called the computer's brain.

95. Which shortcut key is used to copy selected text in MS Word?

  • A. Ctrl + V
  • B. Ctrl + X
  • C. Ctrl + C
  • D. Ctrl + Z
Answer: C — Ctrl + C. Ctrl + C copies; Ctrl + V pastes.

99. The total number of member states of the United Nations is:

  • A. 190
  • B. 193
  • C. 195
  • D. 200

100. The famous K2 mountain is the world's:

  • A. Highest peak
  • B. Second highest peak
