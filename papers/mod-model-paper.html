English — Vocabulary & Grammar

1. Choose the synonym of 'Vigilant':

  • A. Careless
  • B. Watchful
  • C. Sleepy
  • D. Weak
Answer: B — Watchful. Vigilant means watchful and alert.

2. Choose the antonym of 'Conceal':

  • A. Hide
  • B. Reveal
  • C. Cover
  • D. Bury
Answer: B — Reveal. Conceal means hide; reveal is the opposite.

3. The team was trained to act ___ orders.

  • A. on
  • B. upon
  • C. according to
  • D. with of
Answer: C — according to. We say 'according to' orders/instructions.

4. Choose the correct spelling:

  • A. Discipline
  • B. Disipline
  • C. Disciplin
  • D. Disscipline
Answer: A — Discipline. The correct spelling is 'discipline'.

5. One word: 'A soldier who fights for money' is a:

  • A. Volunteer
  • B. Mercenary
  • C. Patriot
  • D. Recruit
Answer: B — Mercenary. A mercenary fights for pay, not for a country.

6. He has served in the army ___ twenty years.

  • A. since
  • B. for
  • C. from
  • D. by
Answer: B — for. 'For' is used with a period of time.

7. Choose the synonym of 'Brave':

  • A. Timid
  • B. Cowardly
  • C. Courageous
  • D. Afraid
Answer: C — Courageous. Brave means courageous.

8. The idiom 'to be on the alert' means:

  • A. To be asleep
  • B. To be ready and watchful
  • C. To be confused
  • D. To be absent
Answer: B — To be ready and watchful. On the alert means watchful and prepared.

9. Choose the correct sentence:

  • A. Each of the soldiers have a rifle.
  • B. Each of the soldiers has a rifle.
  • C. Each of the soldiers are having rifles.
  • D. Each soldier have rifles.
Answer: B — Each of the soldiers has a rifle.. 'Each' is singular, so 'has' is correct.

10. The antonym of 'Victory' is:

  • A. Glory
  • B. Defeat
  • C. Honour
  • D. Success
Answer: B — Defeat. Victory's opposite is defeat.

11. A place where soldiers are housed is called:

  • A. Barracks
  • B. Hangar
  • C. Stable
  • D. Depot only
Answer: A — Barracks. Soldiers live in barracks.

12. He was promoted ___ the rank of Major.

  • A. for
  • B. to
  • C. at
  • D. into of
Answer: B — to. We say 'promoted to' a rank.

13. Choose the synonym of 'Secret':

  • A. Open
  • B. Hidden
  • C. Known
  • D. Public
Answer: B — Hidden. A secret is something hidden.

14. The plural of 'Army' is:

  • A. Armys
  • B. Armies
  • C. Armyes
  • D. Armees
Answer: B — Armies. Words ending in consonant + y change y to 'ies': armies.

15. Information must be passed ___ the proper channel.

  • A. through
  • B. across of
  • C. between of
  • D. among of
Answer: A — through. 'Through the proper channel' is the correct phrase.

Verbal Reasoning

16. Book is to Library as Aeroplane is to:

  • A. Pilot
  • B. Hangar
  • C. Sky
  • D. Runway only
Answer: B — Hangar. Books are kept in a library; aeroplanes are kept in a hangar.

17. Doctor is to Hospital as Teacher is to:

  • A. Student
  • B. Book
  • C. School
  • D. Class only
Answer: C — School. A teacher works in a school.

18. Find the odd one out:

  • A. Apple
  • B. Mango
  • C. Banana
  • D. Potato
Answer: D — Potato. Potato is a vegetable (and grows underground); the others are fruits.

19. If CAT is coded as 3120 (C=3, A=1, T=20), then DOG is:

  • A. 4157
  • B. 4156
  • C. 4158
  • D. 4167
Answer: A — 4157. D=4, O=15, G=7 → 4157.

20. Which word does NOT belong?

  • A. Honest
  • B. Truthful
  • C. Sincere
  • D. Cunning
Answer: D — Cunning. Cunning means sly/deceitful; the others mean honest.

21. Eye is to See as Ear is to:

  • A. Hear
  • B. Listen only
  • C. Sound
  • D. Noise
Answer: A — Hear. Eyes see; ears hear.

22. Find the odd one out (shapes):

  • A. Square
  • B. Circle
  • C. Triangle
  • D. Rectangle
Answer: B — Circle. Circle has no straight sides or corners; the others are polygons.

23. If every letter of a word is replaced by the letter just before it in the alphabet (B→A, C→B, ... A→Z), then FIRE becomes:

  • A. EHQD
  • B. EHRE
  • C. FJSF
  • D. DHQD
Answer: A — EHQD. Each letter moves one step back: F→E, I→H, R→Q, E→D.

24. Pen is to Write as Knife is to:

  • A. Sharp
  • B. Cut
  • C. Steel
  • D. Kitchen
Answer: B — Cut. A knife is used to cut.

25. Which of the following is the odd one out?

  • A. January
  • B. March
  • C. May
  • D. June
Answer: D — June. June has 30 days; the others listed have 31.

26. Bird is to Nest as Bee is to:

  • A. Hive
  • B. Honey
  • C. Flower
  • D. Sting
Answer: A — Hive. A bee lives in a hive.

27. If BOOK = 43 (B=2, O=15, O=15, K=11 — sum of letter positions), then PEN = ?

  • A. 35
  • B. 33
  • C. 30
  • D. 28
Answer: A — 35. P=16, E=5, N=14; 16+5+14 = 35.

28. Clock is to Time as Thermometer is to:

  • A. Heat only
  • B. Temperature
  • C. Weather
  • D. Pressure
Answer: B — Temperature. A thermometer measures temperature.

29. Find the odd one out (materials):

  • A. Copper
  • B. Iron
  • C. Aluminium
  • D. Coal
Answer: D — Coal. Coal is a non-metal (fuel); the others are metals.

30. Ship is to Captain as Army is to:

  • A. Soldier
  • B. General
  • C. Major only
  • D. Troop
Answer: B — General. An army is commanded by a general, as a ship by its captain.

Non-Verbal / Analytical Reasoning

31. What comes next in the series: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ___?

  • A. 35
  • B. 37
  • C. 36
  • D. 40
Answer: B — 37. Differences: 3, 5, 7, 9 — next difference 11 → 37. (Also n²+1.)

32. Complete the series: A, C, F, J, O, ___

  • A. S
  • B. T
  • C. U
  • D. V
Answer: C — U. Jumps of +2, +3, +4, +5 letters; next jump +6 → U.

33. If 5 = 25, 6 = 36, 7 = 49, then 9 = ?

  • A. 81
  • B. 72
  • C. 90
  • D. 99
Answer: A — 81. Each number is squared: 9² = 81.

34. A man walks 4 km North, then 3 km East. How far is he from the starting point?

  • A. 5 km
  • B. 6 km
  • C. 7 km
  • D. 1 km
Answer: A — 5 km. By Pythagoras: √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 km.

35. What comes next: Z, X, V, T, ___?

  • A. S
  • B. R
  • C. Q
  • D. P
Answer: B — R. Letters decrease by 2: next is R.

36. If in a code 'MADRAS' is written as 'NBESBT', how is 'BOMBAY' written? (Each letter +1)

  • A. CPNCBX
  • B. CPNCBZ
  • C. CQNCBZ
  • D. CPOCBZ
Answer: B — CPNCBZ. Shift each letter one forward: B→C, O→P, M→N, B→C, A→B, Y→Z.

37. Find the missing number: 7, 14, 28, 56, ___

  • A. 84
  • B. 112
  • C. 96
  • D. 70
Answer: B — 112. Each term doubles: 56 × 2 = 112.

38. A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. So A is C's:

  • A. Nephew
  • B. Son
  • C. Brother
  • D. Father
Answer: B — Son. A and B are siblings; B is C's daughter, so A is C's son.

39. Which number completes the pattern: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ___?

  • A. 36
  • B. 38
  • C. 40
  • D. 41
Answer: B — 38. Differences: 3, 5, 7, 9 — next 11 → 38.

40. If today is Friday, what day will it be after 15 days?

  • A. Friday
  • B. Saturday
  • C. Sunday
  • D. Monday
Answer: B — Saturday. 15 days = 2 weeks + 1 day → Saturday.

41. Odd one out: 121, 144, 169, 190

  • A. 121
  • B. 144
  • C. 169
  • D. 190
Answer: D — 190. 121, 144, 169 are perfect squares (11², 12², 13²); 190 is not.

42. A clock shows 3:15. The angle between the hands is closest to:

  • A. 0°
  • B. 7.5°
  • C. 15°
  • D. 90°
Answer: B — 7.5°. At 3:15 the minute hand is at 90° and the hour hand has moved 7.5° past 3 → difference ≈ 7.5°.

43. Complete the series: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ___

  • A. 11
  • B. 12
  • C. 13
  • D. 10
Answer: C — 13. Fibonacci: each term is the sum of the previous two → 13.

44. If 'PAPER' is coded as 'OZODQ', the code uses:

  • A. Each letter +1
  • B. Each letter −1
  • C. Each letter +2
  • D. Reverse order
Answer: B — Each letter −1. P→O, A→Z, P→O, E→D, R→Q: every letter shifts back by one.

45. Pointing to a woman, Ali says: 'She is the mother of my mother's only son.' Who is the woman?

  • A. Ali's aunt
  • B. Ali's mother
  • C. Ali's grandmother
  • D. Ali's sister
Answer: C — Ali's grandmother. My mother's only son = Ali himself; the woman is the mother of Ali's mother = his grandmother.

General Knowledge

46. Pakistan's armed forces day 'Defence Day' marks the war of:

  • A. 1948
  • B. 1965
  • C. 1971
  • D. 1999
Answer: B — 1965. Defence Day commemorates the 1965 war.

47. The Pakistan Air Force day is celebrated on:

  • A. 6 September
  • B. 7 September
  • C. 8 September
  • D. 14 August
Answer: B — 7 September. PAF Day is observed on 7 September.

48. The Nishan-e-Haider is Pakistan's:

  • A. Civil award
  • B. Highest military gallantry award
  • C. Sports award
  • D. Literary award
Answer: B — Highest military gallantry award. Nishan-e-Haider is the highest military award for gallantry.

49. How many Nishan-e-Haider have been awarded (to date)?

  • A. 9
  • B. 10
  • C. 11
  • D. 12
Answer: C — 11. Eleven personnel have received the Nishan-e-Haider.

50. The Pakistan Military Academy (PMA) is located at:

  • A. Rawalpindi
  • B. Kakul, Abbottabad
  • C. Risalpur
  • D. Quetta
Answer: B — Kakul, Abbottabad. PMA is at Kakul, near Abbottabad.

51. The Pakistan Naval Academy is located at:

  • A. Karachi (Manora)
  • B. Gwadar
  • C. Ormara
  • D. Pasni
Answer: A — Karachi (Manora). The Pakistan Naval Academy is at Manora, Karachi.

52. The largest military exercise is generally conducted by which service for land warfare?

  • A. Navy
  • B. Army
  • C. Air Force
  • D. Coast Guards
Answer: B — Army. The Army conducts land-warfare exercises.

53. Pakistan's missile 'Shaheen' is a:

  • A. Cruise missile
  • B. Ballistic missile
  • C. Anti-tank missile
  • D. Torpedo
Answer: B — Ballistic missile. Shaheen is a series of ballistic missiles.

54. The Babur is Pakistan's:

  • A. Tank
  • B. Cruise missile
  • C. Fighter jet
  • D. Submarine
Answer: B — Cruise missile. Babur is a subsonic cruise missile.

55. JF-17 Thunder was developed jointly by Pakistan and:

  • A. Turkey
  • B. China
  • C. USA
  • D. Russia
Answer: B — China. The JF-17 was jointly developed with China.

56. The Pakistan Army's headquarters (GHQ) is in:

  • A. Islamabad
  • B. Rawalpindi
  • C. Lahore
  • D. Karachi
Answer: B — Rawalpindi. GHQ is located in Rawalpindi.

57. The ISI is Pakistan's:

  • A. Police force
  • B. Premier intelligence agency
  • C. Border force
  • D. Navy wing
Answer: B — Premier intelligence agency. ISI (Inter-Services Intelligence) is the premier intelligence agency.

58. The first Chief of Army Staff of Pakistan was:

  • A. General Tikka Khan
  • B. General Frank Messervy
  • C. General Ayub Khan
  • D. General Gracey
Answer: B — General Frank Messervy. General Sir Frank Messervy was the first Commander-in-Chief (1947–48).

59. Pakistan's longest-serving military ruler was:

  • A. Ayub Khan
  • B. Zia-ul-Haq
  • C. Pervez Musharraf
  • D. Yahya Khan
Answer: B — Zia-ul-Haq. Zia-ul-Haq ruled about 11 years (1977–1988), the longest military rule.

60. The Siachen Glacier dispute is between Pakistan and:

  • A. China
  • B. India
  • C. Afghanistan
  • D. Iran
Answer: B — India. Siachen is disputed between Pakistan and India.

Pakistan Studies

61. The First World War started in:

  • A. 1912
  • B. 1914
  • C. 1916
  • D. 1918
Answer: B — 1914. WWI began in 1914.

62. The Urdu-Hindi controversy began in:

  • A. 1857
  • B. 1867
  • C. 1877
  • D. 1885
Answer: B — 1867. The Urdu–Hindi controversy erupted in Banaras in 1867.

63. The MAO school at Aligarh was raised to the status of MAO College in:

  • A. 1875
  • B. 1877
  • C. 1882
  • D. 1898
Answer: B — 1877. Sir Syed founded the MAO school in 1875; it became a college in 1877 (later Aligarh Muslim University).

64. The Indian National Congress was founded in:

  • A. 1885
  • B. 1890
  • C. 1905
  • D. 1883
Answer: A — 1885. Congress was founded in 1885 in Bombay.

65. The partition of Bengal took place in:

  • A. 1905
  • B. 1906
  • C. 1911
  • D. 1909
Answer: A — 1905. Bengal was partitioned in 1905 and the decision was annulled in 1911.

66. The title 'Quaid-e-Azam' for Jinnah was popularised after:

  • A. 1937 elections
  • B. Lahore session 1940
  • C. Direct Action Day
  • D. Cabinet Mission
Answer: A — 1937 elections. The title gained wide use after the 1937 provincial elections.

67. Pakistan's first radio station went on air in Karachi in:

  • A. August 1947
  • B. 1948
  • C. 1950
  • D. 1951
Answer: A — August 1947. Radio Pakistan Karachi began on 14 August 1947.

68. The first Governor of the State Bank of Pakistan was:

  • A. Zahid Hussain
  • B. Abdul Qadir
  • C. Shujaat Hasnain
  • D. Ishrat Hussain
Answer: A — Zahid Hussain. Zahid Hussain was the first Governor of SBP.

69. Pakistan's biggest salt mine is at:

  • A. Kalabagh
  • B. Khewra
  • C. Warcha
  • D. Karachi
Answer: B — Khewra. Khewra (Jhelum) is among the world's largest salt mines.

70. The first census in British India was held in:

  • A. 1871
  • B. 1881
  • C. 1901
  • D. 1861
Answer: B — 1881. The first regular census of India was in 1881 (an 1871–72 count preceded it).

Islamiyat

71. The Quran was revealed to the Prophet (PBUH) through the angel:

  • A. Mikail
  • B. Jibreel
  • C. Israfil
  • D. Malik
Answer: B — Jibreel. Hazrat Jibreel (AS) brought the revelations.

72. The night of 'Laylatul Qadr' falls in the last ___ nights of Ramadan:

  • A. Five
  • B. Seven
  • C. Ten (odd nights)
  • D. Three
Answer: C — Ten (odd nights). Laylatul Qadr is sought in the odd nights of the last ten days.

73. The first person to accept Islam among women was:

  • A. Hazrat Aisha (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Khadijah (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Fatimah (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Halima (RA)
Answer: B — Hazrat Khadijah (RA). Hazrat Khadijah (RA) was the first believer.

74. The Prophet (PBUH) migrated to Madinah in:

  • A. 620 CE
  • B. 622 CE
  • C. 624 CE
  • D. 610 CE
Answer: B — 622 CE. The Hijrah took place in 622 CE.

75. Surah Al-Kauthar is the ___ Surah of the Quran:

  • A. Longest
  • B. Shortest
  • C. Last
  • D. First Madani
Answer: B — Shortest. Al-Kauthar (3 verses) is the shortest Surah.

76. The number of Rak'ahs in Witr prayer is:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 4
Answer: C — 3. Witr is prayed as 3 Rak'ahs (in the Hanafi school followed in Pakistan).

77. The first battle in which Muslims used a trench was:

  • A. Badr
  • B. Uhud
  • C. Khandaq
  • D. Khaybar
Answer: C — Khandaq. A trench was dug around Madinah at Khandaq (5 AH).

78. Hazrat Bilal (RA) belonged to:

  • A. Quraysh
  • B. Abyssinia
  • C. Persia
  • D. Yemen
Answer: B — Abyssinia. Hazrat Bilal (RA) was of Abyssinian origin.

79. The 'Year of Sorrow' (Aam-ul-Huzn) was:

  • A. 8 Nabawi
  • B. 10 Nabawi
  • C. 5 Nabawi
  • D. 12 Nabawi
Answer: B — 10 Nabawi. In 10 Nabawi, Hazrat Khadijah (RA) and Abu Talib passed away.

80. The Quran describes itself as 'Hudan' meaning:

  • A. Law
  • B. Guidance
  • C. History
  • D. Light only
Answer: B — Guidance. Hudan means guidance (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:2).

Basic Mathematics

81. What is 30% of 90?

  • A. 21
  • B. 24
  • C. 27
  • D. 30
Answer: C — 27. 30% of 90 = 27.

82. The next prime number after 13 is:

  • A. 15
  • B. 17
  • C. 19
  • D. 14
Answer: B — 17. 17 is the next prime after 13 (15 is composite).

83. If a shirt costs Rs 750 after a 25% discount, and the discount amount is Rs 250, the original price was:

  • A. Rs 900
  • B. Rs 1,000
  • C. Rs 1,100
  • D. Rs 950
Answer: B — Rs 1,000. 750 + 250 = Rs 1,000.

84. What is 9 × 7 + 8?

  • A. 71
  • B. 70
  • C. 135
  • D. 63
Answer: A — 71. 63 + 8 = 71.

85. The fraction 45/60 in its lowest form is:

  • A. 3/4
  • B. 9/12
  • C. 15/20
  • D. 5/6
Answer: A — 3/4. Divide both by 15: 3/4.

86. A 120 km journey takes 2.5 hours. Average speed is:

  • A. 40 km/h
  • B. 48 km/h
  • C. 50 km/h
  • D. 60 km/h
Answer: B — 48 km/h. 120 ÷ 2.5 = 48 km/h.

87. The value of 10% of 10% of 1000 is:

  • A. 100
  • B. 10
  • C. 1
  • D. 50
Answer: B — 10. 10% of 1000 = 100; 10% of 100 = 10.

88. If x − 9 = 16, then x = ?

  • A. 7
  • B. 23
  • C. 25
  • D. 9

89. The sum of angles on a straight line is:

  • A. 90°
  • B. 180°
  • C. 270°
  • D. 360°
Answer: B — 180°. Angles on a straight line total 180°.

90. How many minutes are there in 2.5 hours?

  • A. 120
  • B. 140
  • C. 150
  • D. 160

Everyday Science

  • A. Heart
  • B. Lungs
