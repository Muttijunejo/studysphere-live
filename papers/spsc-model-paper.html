English — Vocabulary & Grammar

1. Choose the synonym of 'Diligent':

  • A. Lazy
  • B. Hardworking
  • C. Careless
  • D. Slow
Answer: B — Hardworking. Diligent means hardworking and careful.

2. Choose the antonym of 'Generous':

  • A. Kind
  • B. Giving
  • C. Stingy
  • D. Noble
Answer: C — Stingy. Generous means willing to give; stingy is the opposite.

3. Choose the correct spelling:

  • A. Neccessary
  • B. Necessary
  • C. Necesary
  • D. Neccesary
Answer: B — Necessary. Necessary: one 'c', double 's'.

4. The committee ___ submitted its report.

  • A. have
  • B. has
  • C. are
  • D. were
Answer: B — has. A committee acting as one unit takes a singular verb (has).

5. He congratulated me ___ my success.

  • A. for
  • B. at
  • C. on
  • D. about
Answer: C — on. We 'congratulate someone on' an achievement.

6. One word: 'A government by the people' is:

  • A. Autocracy
  • B. Democracy
  • C. Monarchy
  • D. Oligarchy
Answer: B — Democracy. Democracy means rule by the people.

7. The idiom 'to burn the midnight oil' means:

  • A. To waste money
  • B. To work or study late at night
  • C. To cook at night
  • D. To get angry
Answer: B — To work or study late at night. It means to work/study late into the night.

8. Choose the synonym of 'Enormous':

  • A. Tiny
  • B. Huge
  • C. Narrow
  • D. Weak
Answer: B — Huge. Enormous means very large or huge.

9. Neither of the two boys ___ done the homework.

  • A. have
  • B. has
  • C. are
  • D. were
Answer: B — has. 'Neither' is singular, so 'has' is correct.

10. The feminine of 'Bachelor' is:

  • A. Spinster
  • B. Bacheloress
  • C. Lady
  • D. Miss only
Answer: A — Spinster. The traditional feminine of bachelor is spinster.

11. He is blind ___ one eye.

  • A. in
  • B. of
  • C. with
  • D. from
Answer: A — in. We say 'blind in one eye' (but 'blind to' a fault).

12. Choose the correct sentence:

  • A. I am senior than him.
  • B. I am senior to him.
  • C. I am more senior than him.
  • D. I am the senior than him.
Answer: B — I am senior to him.. Words like senior/junior take 'to', not 'than'.

13. The plural of 'Phenomenon' is:

  • A. Phenomenons
  • B. Phenomena
  • C. Phenomenas
  • D. Phenomenon
Answer: B — Phenomena. Phenomenon → phenomena (Greek plural).

14. To 'let the cat out of the bag' means:

  • A. To free a pet
  • B. To reveal a secret
  • C. To start a fight
  • D. To tell a lie
Answer: B — To reveal a secret. It means to disclose a secret, usually by accident.

15. She has a great fondness ___ music.

  • A. of
  • B. for
  • C. with
  • D. in
Answer: B — for. 'Fondness for' something is the correct phrase.

16. Choose the antonym of 'Blunt':

  • A. Rude
  • B. Sharp
  • C. Dull
  • D. Plain
Answer: B — Sharp. Blunt (not sharp) has the opposite 'sharp'.

17. The passive voice of 'He will finish the work' is:

  • A. The work will be finished by him.
  • B. The work will finished by him.
  • C. The work is finished by him.
  • D. The work has been finish by him.
Answer: A — The work will be finished by him.. Future passive: will be + past participle.

18. One word: 'That which cannot be heard' is:

  • A. Invisible
  • B. Inaudible
  • C. Illegible
  • D. Incredible
Answer: B — Inaudible. Inaudible means impossible to hear.

19. Bread and butter ___ his only food.

  • A. are
  • B. were
  • C. is
  • D. have
Answer: C — is. 'Bread and butter' as one dish takes a singular verb.

20. Choose the synonym of 'Precise':

  • A. Vague
  • B. Exact
  • C. Rough
  • D. Loose
Answer: B — Exact. Precise means exact and accurate.

General Knowledge

21. The Thar Desert is located mainly in the province of:

  • A. Punjab
  • B. Sindh
  • C. Balochistan
  • D. KPK
Answer: B — Sindh. Most of the Thar lies in Sindh (Tharparkar region) and India's Rajasthan.

22. The ancient city of Mohenjo-daro belongs to which civilization?

  • A. Gandhara
  • B. Indus Valley
  • C. Harappan of Punjab only
  • D. Mauryan
Answer: B — Indus Valley. Mohenjo-daro was a great city of the Indus Valley Civilization.

23. Which is the largest lake of Pakistan (natural freshwater)?

  • A. Keenjhar
  • B. Manchar
  • C. Haleji
  • D. Saiful Muluk
Answer: B — Manchar. Manchar Lake in Sindh is Pakistan's largest natural freshwater lake.

24. The famous 'Chaukhandi Tombs' are located near:

  • A. Lahore
  • B. Karachi
  • C. Multan
  • D. Peshawar
Answer: B — Karachi. The Chaukhandi Tombs are near Karachi on the National Highway.

25. The first railway in the subcontinent (Karachi–Kotri) was opened in:

  • A. 1855
  • B. 1861
  • C. 1870
  • D. 1901
Answer: B — 1861. The Karachi–Kotri railway line opened in 1861.

26. Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai, the famous Sufi poet, wrote in:

  • A. Punjabi
  • B. Sindhi
  • C. Urdu
  • D. Persian only
Answer: B — Sindhi. Shah Latif wrote his poetry (Shah Jo Risalo) in Sindhi.

27. The Makli Necropolis, a UNESCO site, is in:

  • A. Hyderabad
  • B. Thatta
  • C. Sukkur
  • D. Larkana
Answer: B — Thatta. Makli graveyard is near Thatta, Sindh.

28. Pakistan's first female Prime Minister was:

  • A. Fatima Jinnah
  • B. Benazir Bhutto
  • C. Hina Rabbani Khar
  • D. Begum Rana Liaquat
Answer: B — Benazir Bhutto. Benazir Bhutto became PM in 1988 — the first woman PM of a Muslim country.

29. The highest peak of Sindh is in the Kirthar range and is called:

  • A. Gorakh Hill
  • B. Kirthar Peak
  • C. Lakhi Hills
  • D. Karoonjhar
Answer: A — Gorakh Hill. Gorakh Hill (about 1,734 m) is the highest point of Sindh.

30. The Rani Kot Fort, one of the largest forts in the world, is in:

  • A. Jamshoro District
  • B. Dadu old
  • C. Khairpur
  • D. Tharparkar
Answer: A — Jamshoro District. Ranikot Fort is in Jamshoro District, Sindh.

31. The World Bank's headquarters is in:

  • A. New York
  • B. Washington D.C.
  • C. London
  • D. Paris
Answer: B — Washington D.C.. The World Bank is headquartered in Washington, D.C.

32. Which ocean is the smallest?

  • A. Indian
  • B. Atlantic
  • C. Arctic
  • D. Southern
Answer: C — Arctic. The Arctic Ocean is the smallest and shallowest.

33. The currency of Saudi Arabia is:

  • A. Dirham
  • B. Riyal
  • C. Dinar
  • D. Rial
Answer: B — Riyal. Saudi Arabia uses the riyal.

34. How many continents are there in the world?

  • A. 5
  • B. 6
  • C. 7
  • D. 8
Answer: C — 7. There are seven continents.

35. The 'Statue of Liberty' was gifted to the USA by:

  • A. Britain
  • B. France
  • C. Italy
  • D. Spain
Answer: B — France. France gifted the Statue of Liberty in 1886.

36. The normal pH of pure water is:

  • A. 5
  • B. 6
  • C. 7
  • D. 8
Answer: C — 7. Pure water is neutral with pH 7.

37. Which country won the first FIFA World Cup (1930)?

  • A. Brazil
  • B. Uruguay
  • C. Argentina
  • D. Italy
Answer: B — Uruguay. Uruguay hosted and won the first World Cup in 1930.

38. The largest planet in our solar system is:

  • A. Saturn
  • B. Neptune
  • C. Jupiter
  • D. Uranus
Answer: C — Jupiter. Jupiter is the largest planet.

39. The first computer programmer is considered to be:

  • A. Charles Babbage
  • B. Ada Lovelace
  • C. Alan Turing
  • D. Bill Gates
Answer: B — Ada Lovelace. Ada Lovelace wrote the first algorithm for Babbage's engine.

40. Pakistan came into being on 14 August 1947, which according to the Islamic calendar was 27th of:

  • A. Rajab
  • B. Ramadan
  • C. Shawwal
  • D. Zilhaj
Answer: B — Ramadan. 14 August 1947 corresponded to 27 Ramadan 1366 AH.

Pakistan & Sindh Studies

41. The Pakistan Resolution was presented by:

  • A. Quaid-e-Azam
  • B. A.K. Fazlul Huq
  • C. Liaquat Ali Khan
  • D. H.S. Suhrawardy
Answer: B — A.K. Fazlul Huq. A.K. Fazlul Huq moved the Lahore Resolution on 23 March 1940.

42. The 'Day of Deliverance' was observed by the Muslim League in:

  • A. 1937
  • B. 1939
  • C. 1940
  • D. 1942
Answer: B — 1939. It was observed on 22 December 1939 after Congress ministries resigned.

43. The Wardha Scheme (1937) was related to:

  • A. Education
  • B. Land reforms
  • C. Industry
  • D. Defence
Answer: A — Education. The Wardha Scheme was a Congress education scheme opposed by Muslims.

44. Muhammad bin Qasim conquered Sindh in:

  • A. 700 CE
  • B. 712 CE
  • C. 725 CE
  • D. 750 CE
Answer: B — 712 CE. Muhammad bin Qasim defeated Raja Dahir in 712 CE.

45. The first capital of Pakistan was:

  • A. Lahore
  • B. Islamabad
  • C. Karachi
  • D. Rawalpindi
Answer: C — Karachi. Karachi was Pakistan's first capital (1947–1959).

46. The 1962 Constitution of Pakistan was given by:

  • A. Liaquat Ali Khan
  • B. Ayub Khan
  • C. Zulfikar Ali Bhutto
  • D. Iskander Mirza
Answer: B — Ayub Khan. Ayub Khan promulgated the 1962 Constitution.

47. Under the 1973 Constitution, the Prime Minister is elected by the:

  • A. Senate
  • B. National Assembly
  • C. President
  • D. Provincial Assemblies
Answer: B — National Assembly. The National Assembly elects the Prime Minister.

48. The Senate of Pakistan has a term of:

  • A. 4 years
  • B. 5 years
  • C. 6 years
  • D. 3 years
Answer: C — 6 years. Senators serve six-year terms; half retire every three years.

49. The largest dam of Pakistan by storage is:

  • A. Mangla
  • B. Tarbela
  • C. Warsak
  • D. Chashma
Answer: B — Tarbela. Tarbela Dam on the Indus is Pakistan's largest by storage.

50. The Sukkur Barrage was completed in:

  • A. 1925
  • B. 1932
  • C. 1945
  • D. 1955
Answer: B — 1932. Sukkur (Lloyd) Barrage was completed in 1932.

51. The Guddu Barrage is built on which river?

  • A. Chenab
  • B. Indus
  • C. Jhelum
  • D. Ravi
Answer: B — Indus. Guddu Barrage is on the Indus near Kashmore, Sindh.

52. Who was the first Chief Minister of Sindh after independence?

  • A. Muhammad Ayub Khuhro
  • B. Pir Ilahi Bux
  • C. Yusuf Haroon
  • D. Qazi Fazlullah
Answer: A — Muhammad Ayub Khuhro. Muhammad Ayub Khuhro was Sindh's first Chief Minister in 1947.

53. Pakistan's first census was held in:

  • A. 1948
  • B. 1951
  • C. 1961
  • D. 1972
Answer: B — 1951. The first census of Pakistan was conducted in 1951.

54. The 'One Unit' scheme was established in:

  • A. 1954
  • B. 1955
  • C. 1958
  • D. 1962
Answer: B — 1955. One Unit (merging West Pakistan provinces) was created in 1955.

55. The Tashkent Declaration (1966) ended the war between Pakistan and:

  • A. India
  • B. Afghanistan
  • C. Iran
  • D. China
Answer: A — India. The Tashkent Declaration followed the 1965 Pakistan–India war.

Islamiyat

56. The first book of Hadith collection among the Sihah Sitta is:

  • A. Sahih Muslim
  • B. Sahih Bukhari
  • C. Sunan Abu Dawud
  • D. Jami Tirmidhi
Answer: B — Sahih Bukhari. Sahih al-Bukhari ranks first among the six canonical books.

57. The Ghazwa Tabuk was fought in:

  • A. 7 AH
  • B. 8 AH
  • C. 9 AH
  • D. 10 AH
Answer: C — 9 AH. The Tabuk expedition took place in 9 AH.

58. Hazrat Usman (RA) was martyred in:

  • A. 33 AH
  • B. 35 AH
  • C. 37 AH
  • D. 40 AH
Answer: B — 35 AH. Hazrat Usman (RA) was martyred in 35 AH (656 CE).

59. The name of the Prophet's (PBUH) mother was:

  • A. Hazrat Halima (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Amina (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Khadijah (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Fatimah (RA)
Answer: B — Hazrat Amina (RA). His mother was Hazrat Amina bint Wahb (RA).

60. The Qibla was changed from Jerusalem to Makkah in:

  • A. 1 AH
  • B. 2 AH
  • C. 3 AH
  • D. 5 AH
Answer: B — 2 AH. The Qibla changed to the Kaaba in 2 AH.

61. The total number of Ghazwat is generally reported as:

  • A. 17
  • B. 27
  • C. 35
  • D. 12
Answer: B — 27. About 27 Ghazwat (expeditions led by the Prophet PBUH) are commonly reported.

62. Surah Yaseen is called the ___ of the Quran:

  • A. Heart
  • B. Crown
  • C. Light
  • D. Key
Answer: A — Heart. Surah Yaseen is known as the 'Heart of the Quran'.

63. The first Azan was called by:

  • A. Hazrat Umar (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Bilal (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Hamza (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Zaid (RA)
Answer: B — Hazrat Bilal (RA). Hazrat Bilal ibn Rabah (RA) was the first Muazzin.

64. Wudu has how many Fard (obligatory) acts?

  • A. 3
  • B. 4
  • C. 5
  • D. 6
Answer: B — 4. There are four Fard acts in Wudu (Quran 5:6).

65. The longest reign among the Khulafa-e-Rashideen was of:

  • A. Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Umar (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Usman (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Ali (RA)
Answer: C — Hazrat Usman (RA). Hazrat Usman (RA) ruled about 12 years, the longest of the four.

Basic Mathematics

66. What is 25% of 640?

  • A. 140
  • B. 150
  • C. 160
  • D. 180
Answer: C — 160. 25% of 640 = 640 ÷ 4 = 160.

67. The average of first five natural numbers is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5
Answer: B — 3. (1+2+3+4+5) ÷ 5 = 15 ÷ 5 = 3.

68. If 8 pens cost Rs 200, the cost of 5 pens is:

  • A. Rs 100
  • B. Rs 120
  • C. Rs 125
  • D. Rs 150
Answer: C — Rs 125. One pen = Rs 25; 5 pens = Rs 125.

69. What is the sum of the angles of a triangle?

  • A. 90°
  • B. 180°
  • C. 270°
  • D. 360°
Answer: B — 180°. Angles of any triangle add up to 180°.

70. A bus covers 240 km in 6 hours. Its speed is:

  • A. 30 km/h
  • B. 36 km/h
  • C. 40 km/h
  • D. 48 km/h
Answer: C — 40 km/h. 240 ÷ 6 = 40 km/h.

71. The value of 15² is:

  • A. 215
  • B. 225
  • C. 235
  • D. 250
Answer: B — 225. 15 × 15 = 225.

72. If a number is divisible by both 2 and 3, it is also divisible by:

  • A. 4
  • B. 5
  • C. 6
  • D. 9
Answer: C — 6. LCM of 2 and 3 is 6.

73. The profit on selling a Rs 900 item for Rs 990 is:

  • A. 9%
  • B. 10%
  • C. 11%
  • D. 12%
Answer: B — 10%. Profit = 90; 90 ÷ 900 × 100 = 10%.

74. What comes next: 5, 10, 20, 40, ___?

  • A. 50
  • B. 60
  • C. 80
  • D. 100
Answer: C — 80. Each term doubles; next is 80.

75. The perimeter of a square of side 9 cm is:

  • A. 36 cm
  • B. 81 cm
  • C. 27 cm
  • D. 45 cm
Answer: A — 36 cm. Perimeter = 4 × 9 = 36 cm.

Everyday Science

76. Which vitamin is essential for blood clotting?

  • A. Vitamin A
  • B. Vitamin C
  • C. Vitamin D
  • D. Vitamin K
Answer: D — Vitamin K. Vitamin K is needed for normal blood clotting.

77. The main source of energy for the Earth is:

  • A. Moon
  • B. Sun
  • C. Wind
  • D. Ocean
Answer: B — Sun. The Sun is Earth's primary energy source.

78. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?

  • A. Iron
  • B. Mercury
  • C. Copper
  • D. Aluminium
Answer: B — Mercury. Mercury is the only metal liquid at room temperature.

79. The human heart has how many chambers?

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5
Answer: C — 4. The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

80. Which gas is used in balloons to make them float?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon dioxide
  • C. Helium
  • D. Nitrogen
Answer: C — Helium. Helium is light and non-flammable, so balloons float.

81. Photosynthesis releases which gas into the air?

  • A. Carbon dioxide
  • B. Oxygen
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Methane
Answer: B — Oxygen. Plants release oxygen during photosynthesis.

82. The SI unit of force is:

  • A. Joule
  • B. Newton
  • C. Pascal
  • D. Watt
Answer: B — Newton. Force is measured in newtons.

83. Which part of the eye controls the amount of light entering it?

  • A. Lens
  • B. Retina
  • C. Iris
  • D. Cornea
Answer: C — Iris. The iris adjusts the pupil size to control light.

84. Water covers approximately what percentage of Earth's surface?

  • A. 50%
  • B. 60%
  • C. 71%
  • D. 80%
Answer: C — 71%. About 71% of Earth's surface is water.

85. The disease 'malaria' is spread by:

  • A. Housefly
  • B. Mosquito
  • C. Rat
  • D. Dog
Answer: B — Mosquito. Malaria spreads through the bite of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.

Current Affairs (Stable Facts)

86. Pakistan's currency is managed by the:

  • A. Ministry of Finance
  • B. State Bank of Pakistan
  • C. National Bank
  • D. SECP
Answer: B — State Bank of Pakistan. The State Bank of Pakistan is the central bank.

87. The Diamer-Basha Dam is being built on which river?

  • A. Jhelum
  • B. Indus
  • C. Chenab
  • D. Swat
Answer: B — Indus. Diamer-Basha Dam is on the Indus in Gilgit-Baltistan.

88. Pakistan hosted the SCO Heads of Government meeting in Islamabad in:

  • A. 2022
  • B. 2023
  • C. 2024
  • D. 2025
Answer: C — 2024. Islamabad hosted the SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting in October 2024.

89. The national dish often cited as Pakistan's most popular is:

  • A. Biryani
  • B. Nihari
  • C. Haleem
  • D. Karahi
Answer: A — Biryani. Biryani is widely regarded as Pakistan's most popular dish.

90. Pakistan's largest stock exchange is the:

  • A. Lahore Stock Exchange
  • B. Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX)
  • C. Islamabad Stock Exchange
  • D. Karachi Cotton Exchange
Answer: B — Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). PSX was formed by merging the Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad exchanges (2016).

91. The 'Thar Coal' project is in which district?

  • A. Badin
  • B. Tharparkar
  • C. Sanghar
  • D. Umerkot
Answer: B — Tharparkar. Thar coalfields are in Tharparkar District, Sindh.

92. The United Nations Security Council has how many permanent members?

  • A. 4
  • B. 5
  • C. 6
  • D. 10
Answer: B — 5. There are five permanent members (P5) with veto power.

93. Pakistan's first satellite, Badr-1, was launched in:

  • A. 1988
  • B. 1990
  • C. 1992
  • D. 1994
Answer: B — 1990. Badr-1, Pakistan's first indigenously built satellite, launched in 1990.

94. The Kartarpur Corridor connects Pakistan with:

  • A. India
  • B. Iran
  • C. Afghanistan
  • D. China
Answer: A — India. The Kartarpur Corridor (opened 2019) links to India for Sikh pilgrims.

95. The population of Pakistan, per the 2023 census, is about:

  • A. 220 million
  • B. 240 million
  • C. 260 million
  • D. 200 million
Answer: B — 240 million. The 2023 census counted about 240 million people.

Basic Computer

96. Which of these is output device?

  • A. Mouse
  • B. Scanner
  • C. Monitor
  • D. Keyboard
Answer: C — Monitor. A monitor displays output to the user.

97. The full form of USB is:

  • A. Universal Serial Bus
  • B. United System Bus
  • C. Universal Service Board
  • D. Unique Serial Bus
Answer: A — Universal Serial Bus. USB stands for Universal Serial Bus.

98. In MS Word, the shortcut for making text bold is:

  • A. Ctrl + I
  • B. Ctrl + B
  • C. Ctrl + U
  • D. Ctrl + E
Answer: B — Ctrl + B. Ctrl + B applies bold formatting.

99. Which memory is volatile?

  • A. ROM
  • B. Hard disk
  • C. RAM
  • D. SSD
Answer: C — RAM. RAM loses its data when power is switched off.

100. The 'Ctrl + P' shortcut is used to:

  • A. Paste
  • B. Print
  • C. Preview only
  • D. Protect
Answer: B — Print. Ctrl + P opens the print dialog.
