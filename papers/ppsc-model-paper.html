English — Vocabulary & Grammar

1. Choose the synonym of 'Candid':

  • A. Clever
  • B. Frank
  • C. Rude
  • D. Shy
Answer: B — Frank. Candid means frank and honest in speech.

2. Choose the antonym of 'Scarce':

  • A. Rare
  • B. Abundant
  • C. Costly
  • D. Little
Answer: B — Abundant. Scarce means in short supply; abundant is its opposite.

3. The correct spelling is:

  • A. Embarass
  • B. Embarrass
  • C. Embarras
  • D. Emmbarass
Answer: B — Embarrass. Embarrass has double 'r' and double 's'.

4. He is suffering ___ fever.

  • A. with
  • B. from
  • C. by
  • D. of
Answer: B — from. We say 'suffer from' an illness.

5. Choose the correct sentence:

  • A. She has went home.
  • B. She has gone home.
  • C. She have gone home.
  • D. She gone home.
Answer: B — She has gone home.. Present perfect: has + past participle (gone).

6. One word: 'A place where birds are kept' is an:

  • A. Apiary
  • B. Aviary
  • C. Aquarium
  • D. Stable
Answer: B — Aviary. An aviary is a large enclosure for birds; an apiary is for bees.

7. The idiom 'a blessing in disguise' means:

  • A. A clear loss
  • B. A hidden benefit
  • C. A religious event
  • D. A secret plan
Answer: B — A hidden benefit. It means something good that first seemed bad.

8. He ran fast ___ miss the train.

  • A. so as not to
  • B. so that
  • C. because
  • D. in order
Answer: A — so as not to. 'So as not to' expresses a negative purpose.

9. Choose the antonym of 'Permanent':

  • A. Stable
  • B. Temporary
  • C. Fixed
  • D. Lasting
Answer: B — Temporary. Permanent means lasting forever; temporary is the opposite.

10. The plural of 'Criterion' is:

  • A. Criterions
  • B. Criteria
  • C. Criterias
  • D. Criterion
Answer: B — Criteria. Criterion (Greek origin) has the plural 'criteria'.

11. I have been waiting here ___ two hours.

  • A. since
  • B. from
  • C. for
  • D. by
Answer: C — for. 'For' is used with a length of time (two hours).

12. Choose the synonym of 'Hostile':

  • A. Friendly
  • B. Unfriendly
  • C. Neutral
  • D. Kind
Answer: B — Unfriendly. Hostile means unfriendly or opposed.

13. The passive voice of 'They built a bridge' is:

  • A. A bridge is built by them.
  • B. A bridge was built by them.
  • C. A bridge has building by them.
  • D. A bridge built them.
Answer: B — A bridge was built by them.. Past simple passive: was + past participle (built).

14. A 'red-letter day' means:

  • A. A sad day
  • B. An important, happy day
  • C. A holiday
  • D. A working day
Answer: B — An important, happy day. A red-letter day is a special, memorable day.

15. She prefers tea ___ coffee.

  • A. than
  • B. from
  • C. to
  • D. over than
Answer: C — to. We say 'prefer X to Y'.

General Knowledge

16. The capital of Australia is:

  • A. Sydney
  • B. Melbourne
  • C. Canberra
  • D. Perth
Answer: C — Canberra. Canberra is the capital; Sydney is the largest city.

17. The first woman to win a Nobel Prize was:

  • A. Marie Curie
  • B. Florence Nightingale
  • C. Mother Teresa
  • D. Jane Addams
Answer: A — Marie Curie. Marie Curie won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903.

18. Which is the smallest planet in our solar system?

  • A. Venus
  • B. Mars
  • C. Mercury
  • D. Pluto
Answer: C — Mercury. Mercury is the smallest of the eight planets (Pluto is a dwarf planet).

19. The World War II ended in:

  • A. 1943
  • B. 1944
  • C. 1945
  • D. 1947
Answer: C — 1945. WWII ended in 1945.

20. The biggest island in the world is:

  • A. Borneo
  • B. Greenland
  • C. Sumatra
  • D. Madagascar
Answer: B — Greenland. Greenland is the world's largest island.

21. The founder of the theory of evolution by natural selection was:

  • A. Newton
  • B. Darwin
  • C. Einstein
  • D. Pasteur
Answer: B — Darwin. Charles Darwin proposed evolution by natural selection.

22. The national language of Brazil is:

  • A. Spanish
  • B. Portuguese
  • C. French
  • D. Italian
Answer: B — Portuguese. Brazil was a Portuguese colony, so Portuguese is official.

23. How many players are there in a cricket team on the field?

  • A. 10
  • B. 11
  • C. 12
  • D. 9
Answer: B — 11. A cricket team fields 11 players.

24. The hardest naturally occurring substance is:

  • A. Iron
  • B. Gold
  • C. Diamond
  • D. Quartz
Answer: C — Diamond. Diamond is the hardest natural material.

25. The currency of China is:

  • A. Yen
  • B. Won
  • C. Yuan (Renminbi)
  • D. Dollar
Answer: C — Yuan (Renminbi). China's currency is the yuan (renminbi).

26. The Taj Mahal is located in:

  • A. Delhi
  • B. Jaipur
  • C. Agra
  • D. Lucknow
Answer: C — Agra. The Taj Mahal stands in Agra, built by Shah Jahan.

27. The first Olympic Games (modern) were held in:

  • A. 1892
  • B. 1896
  • C. 1900
  • D. 1904
Answer: B — 1896. The first modern Olympics were held in Athens in 1896.

28. Which country has the largest population in the world?

  • A. China
  • B. India
  • C. USA
  • D. Indonesia
Answer: B — India. India overtook China as the most populous country (UN estimates, 2023).

29. The chemical symbol for sodium is:

  • A. So
  • B. Sd
  • C. Na
  • D. S
Answer: C — Na. Na comes from 'natrium', the Latin name of sodium.

30. The Mount Everest lies in which mountain range?

  • A. Karakoram
  • B. Hindu Kush
  • C. Himalayas
  • D. Andes
Answer: C — Himalayas. Everest is in the Himalayas on the Nepal–China border.

31. The author of 'Hamlet' is:

  • A. Charles Dickens
  • B. William Shakespeare
  • C. John Milton
  • D. Jane Austen
Answer: B — William Shakespeare. Shakespeare wrote Hamlet.

32. The largest mammal in the world is:

  • A. African elephant
  • B. Blue whale
  • C. Giraffe
  • D. Hippo
Answer: B — Blue whale. The blue whale is the largest animal known.

33. Which gas is most responsible for the greenhouse effect among these?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Nitrogen
  • C. Carbon dioxide
  • D. Argon
Answer: C — Carbon dioxide. Carbon dioxide is the main long-lived greenhouse gas from human activity.

34. The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with:

  • A. Red Sea
  • B. Gulf of Oman
  • C. Arabian Sea directly
  • D. Caspian Sea
Answer: B — Gulf of Oman. Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman.

35. The United Nations was founded in:

  • A. 1919
  • B. 1939
  • C. 1945
  • D. 1950
Answer: C — 1945. The UN was founded in 1945 after WWII.

Pakistan Studies

36. Allama Iqbal presented his famous Allahabad Address in:

  • A. 1928
  • B. 1930
  • C. 1932
  • D. 1940
Answer: B — 1930. Iqbal's Allahabad Address was delivered in December 1930.

37. The Khilafat Movement was launched to protect:

  • A. Turkey's Caliphate
  • B. Indian Muslims' jobs
  • C. Urdu language
  • D. Separate electorates
Answer: A — Turkey's Caliphate. The Khilafat Movement (1919–1924) supported the Ottoman Caliphate.

38. The Cripps Mission came to India in:

  • A. 1940
  • B. 1942
  • C. 1945
  • D. 1946
Answer: B — 1942. The Cripps Mission arrived in March 1942.

39. The Cabinet Mission Plan was announced in:

  • A. 1942
  • B. 1945
  • C. 1946
  • D. 1947
Answer: C — 1946. The Cabinet Mission came to India in 1946.

40. Quaid-e-Azam joined the Muslim League in:

  • A. 1906
  • B. 1913
  • C. 1916
  • D. 1920
Answer: B — 1913. Jinnah joined the All-India Muslim League in 1913.

41. The first session of the Constituent Assembly of Pakistan was held on:

  • A. 10 August 1947
  • B. 14 August 1947
  • C. 11 August 1947
  • D. 15 August 1947
Answer: A — 10 August 1947. The first session met on 10 August 1947 in Karachi.

42. Pakistan's first general elections were held in:

  • A. 1958
  • B. 1970
  • C. 1977
  • D. 1985
Answer: B — 1970. The first nationwide general elections were held in December 1970.

43. The capital of Pakistan was shifted from Karachi to Islamabad in the:

  • A. 1950s
  • B. 1960s
  • C. 1970s
  • D. 1980s
Answer: B — 1960s. Islamabad became the capital during the 1960s under Ayub Khan.

44. The Pakistan–China border agreement was signed in:

  • A. 1960
  • B. 1963
  • C. 1965
  • D. 1972
Answer: B — 1963. The boundary agreement with China was signed in 1963.

45. Pakistan's first nuclear tests were carried out in:

  • A. May 1998
  • B. May 1999
  • C. June 1998
  • D. April 1998
Answer: A — May 1998. Pakistan conducted nuclear tests at Chagai in May 1998.

46. The longest river of Pakistan is:

  • A. Jhelum
  • B. Chenab
  • C. Indus
  • D. Beas
Answer: C — Indus. The Indus (about 3,180 km total) is Pakistan's main and longest river.

47. Pakistan's national poet is:

  • A. Faiz Ahmed Faiz
  • B. Allama Muhammad Iqbal
  • C. Ahmed Faraz
  • D. Mir Taqi Mir
Answer: B — Allama Muhammad Iqbal. Allama Iqbal is the national poet (Shair-e-Mashriq).

48. The Badshahi Mosque in Lahore was built by:

  • A. Shah Jahan
  • B. Akbar
  • C. Aurangzeb
  • D. Jahangir
Answer: C — Aurangzeb. Aurangzeb built the Badshahi Mosque in 1673.

49. Mohenjo-daro is located in the district of:

  • A. Sukkur
  • B. Larkana
  • C. Hyderabad
  • D. Thatta
Answer: B — Larkana. Mohenjo-daro is in Larkana District, Sindh.

50. The first martial law in Pakistan was imposed in:

  • A. 1956
  • B. 1958
  • C. 1969
  • D. 1977
Answer: B — 1958. Ayub Khan imposed martial law in October 1958.

Islamiyat

51. The total number of verses (Ayahs) in the Holy Quran is:

  • A. 6,236
  • B. 6,666
  • C. 6,000
  • D. 6,348
Answer: A — 6,236. The commonly cited count of Quranic verses is 6,236 (excluding Basmalah repetitions).

52. Surah Al-Ikhlas is also known as the Surah of:

  • A. Creation
  • B. Tawhid (Oneness of Allah)
  • C. Patience
  • D. Charity
Answer: B — Tawhid (Oneness of Allah). Al-Ikhlas declares the absolute Oneness of Allah.

53. The Battle of Uhud took place in:

  • A. 2 AH
  • B. 3 AH
  • C. 4 AH
  • D. 6 AH
Answer: B — 3 AH. Uhud was fought in Shawwal, 3 AH.

54. How many times is the word 'Quran' mentioned in the Quran (approx.)?

  • A. 50
  • B. 70
  • C. 100
  • D. 40
Answer: B — 70. The word 'Quran' appears about 70 times in the Quran.

55. The first female martyr (Shaheed) in Islam was:

  • A. Hazrat Khadijah (RA)
  • B. Hazrat Sumayyah (RA)
  • C. Hazrat Fatimah (RA)
  • D. Hazrat Aisha (RA)
Answer: B — Hazrat Sumayyah (RA). Hazrat Sumayyah (RA) was the first martyr in Islam.

56. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah was signed in:

  • A. 5 AH
  • B. 6 AH
  • C. 7 AH
  • D. 8 AH
Answer: B — 6 AH. Hudaybiyyah took place in 6 AH (628 CE).

57. The conquest of Makkah occurred in:

  • A. 6 AH
  • B. 7 AH
  • C. 8 AH
  • D. 10 AH
Answer: C — 8 AH. Makkah was conquered peacefully in Ramadan, 8 AH.

58. The Farewell Sermon (Khutbatul Wida) was delivered in:

  • A. 9 AH
  • B. 10 AH
  • C. 11 AH
  • D. 8 AH
Answer: B — 10 AH. The Farewell Sermon was delivered during Hajj in 10 AH.

59. How many Hajj did the Prophet (PBUH) perform?

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. Four
Answer: A — One. The Prophet (PBUH) performed one Hajj (the Farewell Hajj).

60. The number of Umrahs performed by the Prophet (PBUH) was:

  • A. Two
  • B. Three
  • C. Four
  • D. Five
Answer: C — Four. The Prophet (PBUH) performed four Umrahs.

61. Hazrat Umar (RA) became Caliph in:

  • A. 11 AH
  • B. 13 AH
  • C. 15 AH
  • D. 17 AH
Answer: B — 13 AH. Hazrat Umar (RA) succeeded in 13 AH (634 CE).

62. The compilation of Hadith 'Sahih Bukhari' was done by:

  • A. Imam Muslim
  • B. Imam Bukhari
  • C. Imam Tirmidhi
  • D. Imam Abu Dawud
Answer: B — Imam Bukhari. Imam Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari compiled Sahih al-Bukhari.

63. The angel who brings revelation (Wahi) is:

  • A. Mikail
  • B. Israfil
  • C. Jibreel (Gabriel)
  • D. Izrail
Answer: C — Jibreel (Gabriel). Hazrat Jibreel (AS) brought the revelations.

64. The first migration (Hijrah) of Muslims was to:

  • A. Madinah
  • B. Taif
  • C. Abyssinia (Habsha)
  • D. Yemen
Answer: C — Abyssinia (Habsha). The first migration was to Abyssinia in 5 Nabawi (615 CE).

65. Ramadan is the ___ month of the Islamic calendar:

  • A. 8th
  • B. 9th
  • C. 10th
  • D. 7th
Answer: B — 9th. Ramadan is the ninth month of the Hijri calendar.

Basic Mathematics

66. What is 40% of 250?

  • A. 80
  • B. 90
  • C. 100
  • D. 110
Answer: C — 100. 40% of 250 = 0.40 × 250 = 100.

67. If x + 7 = 15, then x = ?

  • A. 6
  • B. 7
  • C. 8
  • D. 9
Answer: C — 8. x = 15 − 7 = 8.

68. The LCM of 6 and 8 is:

  • A. 12
  • B. 24
  • C. 48
  • D. 16
Answer: B — 24. Multiples of 6 and 8 first meet at 24.

69. A shopkeeper sells an article at a loss of 10%. If its cost price is Rs 500, the selling price is:

  • A. Rs 450
  • B. Rs 460
  • C. Rs 490
  • D. Rs 510
Answer: A — Rs 450. SP = 500 − 10% of 500 = 500 − 50 = Rs 450.

70. The square root of 144 is:

  • A. 11
  • B. 12
  • C. 14
  • D. 16
Answer: B — 12. 12 × 12 = 144.

71. What is the value of 7 × 8 − 6 ÷ 2?

  • A. 25
  • B. 52
  • C. 53
  • D. 28
Answer: C — 53. BODMAS: 56 − 3 = 53.

72. The ratio 2:3 expressed as a percentage (first part of total) is:

  • A. 50%
  • B. 40%
  • C. 66.7%
  • D. 60%
Answer: B — 40%. Total parts = 5; 2/5 = 40%.

73. If a car covers 90 km in 1.5 hours, its speed is:

  • A. 45 km/h
  • B. 60 km/h
  • C. 75 km/h
  • D. 135 km/h
Answer: B — 60 km/h. Speed = 90 ÷ 1.5 = 60 km/h.

74. A number increased by 25% becomes 500. The original number is:

  • A. 375
  • B. 400
  • C. 425
  • D. 450
Answer: B — 400. 125% of x = 500 → x = 500 ÷ 1.25 = 400.

75. The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm is:

  • A. 30 cm²
  • B. 60 cm²
  • C. 16 cm²
  • D. 36 cm²
Answer: A — 30 cm². Area = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm².

Everyday Science

76. The unit of electric current is:

  • A. Volt
  • B. Ohm
  • C. Ampere
  • D. Watt
Answer: C — Ampere. Current is measured in amperes.

77. Which part of the plant absorbs water from the soil?

  • A. Leaf
  • B. Stem
  • C. Root
  • D. Flower
Answer: C — Root. Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil.

78. The boiling point of water at sea level is:

  • A. 90°C
  • B. 100°C
  • C. 110°C
  • D. 80°C
Answer: B — 100°C. Water boils at 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure.

79. Deficiency of iron in the body causes:

  • A. Scurvy
  • B. Anaemia
  • C. Rickets
  • D. Goitre
Answer: B — Anaemia. Iron deficiency leads to anaemia (low haemoglobin).

80. Which instrument is used to measure atmospheric pressure?

  • A. Thermometer
  • B. Barometer
  • C. Hygrometer
  • D. Anemometer
Answer: B — Barometer. A barometer measures air pressure.

81. The planet nearest to the Sun is:

  • A. Venus
  • B. Earth
  • C. Mercury
  • D. Mars
Answer: C — Mercury. Mercury is closest to the Sun.

82. Which of these is a renewable source of energy?

  • A. Coal
  • B. Oil
  • C. Solar energy
  • D. Natural gas
Answer: C — Solar energy. Solar energy is renewable; fossil fuels are not.

83. The main gas in the air that plants use to make food is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon dioxide
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Hydrogen
Answer: B — Carbon dioxide. Plants use carbon dioxide for photosynthesis.

84. How many bones are there in an adult human body?

  • A. 196
  • B. 206
  • C. 216
  • D. 226
Answer: B — 206. An adult human has 206 bones.

85. The chemical formula of methane is:

  • A. CH₄
  • B. CO₂
  • C. C₂H₆
  • D. NH₃
Answer: A — CH₄. Methane is CH₄ — the main part of natural gas.

Basic Computer

86. The full form of RAM is:

  • A. Random Access Memory
  • B. Read Access Memory
  • C. Rapid Action Machine
  • D. Remote Access Module
Answer: A — Random Access Memory. RAM stands for Random Access Memory.

87. Which device is used to print documents?

  • A. Scanner
  • B. Printer
  • C. Monitor
  • D. Plotter only
Answer: B — Printer. A printer produces hard copies of documents.

88. In MS Excel, a file is called a:

  • A. Document
  • B. Workbook
  • C. Slide
  • D. Sheet only
Answer: B — Workbook. An Excel file is a workbook containing worksheets.

89. The smallest unit of data in a computer is:

  • A. Byte
  • B. Bit
  • C. KB
  • D. Nibble
Answer: B — Bit. A bit (0 or 1) is the smallest unit of data.

90. Which key combination is used to 'Undo' in most Windows applications?

  • A. Ctrl + Y
  • B. Ctrl + Z
  • C. Ctrl + U
  • D. Alt + Z
Answer: B — Ctrl + Z. Ctrl + Z undoes the last action.

91. WWW stands for:

  • A. World Web Wide
  • B. World Wide Web
  • C. Wide World Web
  • D. Web World Wide
Answer: B — World Wide Web. WWW = World Wide Web.

92. Which of the following is an operating system?

  • A. MS Word
  • B. Windows 11
  • C. Google Chrome
  • D. Photoshop
Answer: B — Windows 11. Windows 11 is an operating system; the others are applications.

93. A computer virus is a:

  • A. Hardware fault
  • B. Malicious program
  • C. Type of battery
  • D. Network cable
Answer: B — Malicious program. A virus is a harmful program that spreads between computers.

94. The default extension of an MS Word document (new versions) is:

  • A. .doc
  • B. .docx
  • C. .txt
  • D. .pdf
Answer: B — .docx. Word documents use .docx since Word 2007.

95. Which storage device is known as permanent memory?

  • A. RAM
  • B. Cache
  • C. Hard disk
  • D. Register
Answer: C — Hard disk. The hard disk stores data permanently, even when power is off.

Current Affairs (Stable Facts)

  • B. 2017

  • A. 2013
  • B. 2015

98. Pakistan's national animal is:

  • A. Markhor
  • B. Snow leopard

  • A. India
  • B. China
  • C. Afghanistan

100. The 1973 Constitution has how many articles (originally)?

  • A. 280
  • B. 290
  • C. 300
  • D. 342
Answer: A — 280. The 1973 Constitution originally contained 280 articles.
