Privacy Policy

Aakhri update: 28 November 2026

Koi account nahi, koi personal data nahi

StudySphere par koi account banane ki zaroorat nahi, aur website aap ka naam, number ya koi personal maloomat collect nahi karti.

Visit counting

Website par kitne log aye, ye ginti GoatCounter se hoti hai — ye sirf anonymous visit counts rakhta hai, kisi visitor ki pehchan ya tracking nahi hoti.

Aap ke test scores

Test Centre ke scores aur history sirf aap ke apne browser mein save hote hain (localStorage) — wo hum tak nahi pohanchte, aur browser data saaf karne se mit jate hain.

External links

Website par official idaron (SPSC, HEC, scholarship programmes waghera) ke links hain — un websites ki apni privacy policies hoti hain.
